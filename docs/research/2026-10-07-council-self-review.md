# Universal Council — External Research & Community Review
Date: 2026-10-07
Status: PROPOSALS PRESENTED TO COUNCIL — NOT YET MERGED INTO GOVERNANCE

## Scope
All roles used for commercial-project selection reviewed comparable methods, professional guidance, recent research, and practitioner communities. The goal was not to collect fashionable frameworks, but to find concrete ways to reduce premature closure, groupthink, weak validation, and unnecessary process.

## Role-by-role findings

### Chair / Decision Controller
Comparable ideas:
- DACI / RAPID decision-rights models.
- Lightweight decision records.

Proposal:
1. Every material decision gets one explicit Decision Owner.
2. Separate Driver from final Approver.
3. Record: question, options, rejected options, evidence, owner, expiry/review trigger.
4. Do not seek consensus when authority and evidence are already sufficient.

### Evidence & Research Lead
Comparable ideas:
- Analysis of Competing Hypotheses (ACH).
- Evidence-weighted risk/governance frameworks.

Proposal:
1. Build evidence against all live hypotheses, not only for the leading one.
2. Tag evidence as supporting, contradicting, or non-discriminating.
3. Require source diversity for pivotal claims.
4. Add confidence and evidence freshness to material findings.

### Red Team / Devil's Advocate
Comparable ideas:
- Premortem.
- Adversarial collaboration.

Proposal:
1. Mandatory premortem before SELECTED for non-trivial projects.
2. Red Team must state what evidence would falsify its own objection.
3. Red Team receives the proposal only after producing an independent failure list for high-impact decisions.

### Practicality & Execution Reviewer
Comparable ideas:
- Opportunity Solution Tree / continuous discovery.
- Assumption testing before build.

Proposal:
1. Convert every project into Outcome → Opportunity → Solution → Assumption Test.
2. Find the cheapest test that can kill the riskiest assumption.
3. Ban build work that does not test a stated assumption or unlock a validated workflow.

### Risk & Consequence Analyst
Comparable ideas:
- FMEA.
- NIST Govern / Map / Measure / Manage.

Proposal:
1. Use lightweight failure-mode analysis for material ventures.
2. Score severity, likelihood, detectability, and reversibility.
3. Distinguish risk that blocks launch from risk that can be monitored after launch.
4. Add explicit stop-loss / kill triggers.

### Human / User Advocate
Comparable ideas:
- Continuous customer discovery.
- Practitioner guidance emphasizing past behavior over hypothetical intent.

Proposal:
1. Prefer observed/past behavior over stated future intent.
2. Represent both User and Economic Buyer when they differ.
3. Add workflow burden: what new work does the solution create for the customer?
4. Reject “users said they liked it” as validation without behavior or commitment.

### Cost-Benefit & Value Analyst
Comparable ideas:
- Gabor-Granger / Van Westendorp pricing research.
- Paid-pilot practitioner validation.

Proposal:
1. Separate “value exists” from “price is right.”
2. Use a willingness-to-pay ladder after problem validation.
3. Treat budget, paid pilot, deposit, signed LOI with real internal cost, or workflow access as stronger evidence than praise.
4. Track value created in money/time/risk, not feature count.

### Synthesis & Alternatives Designer
Comparable ideas:
- Delphi / Nominal Group Technique.
- Consensus-free and diversity-aware multi-agent debate.

Proposal:
1. Independent first-round positions before seeing peer views.
2. Anonymous/blinded arguments in ARENA where identity can anchor judgment.
3. Preserve minority position if it has stronger evidence.
4. Synthesis optimizes truth and decision quality, not convergence.

### Business & Operations Specialist
Comparable ideas:
- Continuous discovery and outcome-based product framing.
- Practitioner paid-pilot methods.

Proposal:
1. Explicitly separate User, Buyer, Budget Owner, and Approver.
2. Require current-workaround economics before solution economics.
3. Add adoption path: who changes behavior and what process must change?
4. Prefer a narrow paid slice over a large free pilot.

### Customer Skeptic
Comparable ideas:
- Customer discovery focused on past behavior and existing alternatives.
- Pre-sale / paid-pilot validation.

Proposal:
1. Add a “Do Nothing” competitor to every commercial analysis.
2. Ask what the buyer did the last time the problem happened.
3. Ask what they already spend—money, time, staff, risk—on the workaround.
4. Positive interest is weak evidence; behavior and commitment are strong evidence.
5. Free pilot must be narrow and explicitly time-bounded.

### Commoditization & Moat Auditor
Comparable ideas:
- Current AI-startup discussions emphasize distribution, workflow lock-in, proprietary data, relationships, integration, and switching costs rather than generation capability itself.

Proposal:
1. Add “Foundation Model Uplift Test”: if base AI becomes 10x better and cheaper, does the venture improve, survive, or die?
2. Separate technical moat from operational moat.
3. Require at least one credible accumulating advantage before scale: data, workflow history, integration, distribution, relationships, regulatory position, brand/trust, or switching cost.
4. Treat “we use AI” and “we built it first” as zero moat.

## Council-level research findings

Recent multi-agent debate research suggests:
- vanilla debate can underperform simpler aggregation;
- homogeneous agents can preserve the same error rather than correct it;
- majority pressure can suppress a correct minority;
- diversity of initial positions matters;
- calibrated confidence can improve updating;
- prolonged debate can add cost/noise;
- consensus is not inherently desirable;
- selective debate is more efficient than debating everything;
- identity can cause sycophancy and self-bias.

## Proposed Council Protocol 0.3 changes

### P1 — Blind First Position
For ARENA and high-impact COUNCIL work, required voices produce their first position before seeing other positions.

### P2 — Confidence Field
Every material position carries:
- confidence: 0–100
- pivotal unknowns
- what evidence would change the position

Confidence is not a vote weight by itself; it is a calibration signal.

### P3 — Evidence Matrix
For contested decisions, maintain a compact option × evidence matrix:
- supports
- contradicts
- non-discriminating
- unknown

### P4 — Selective Debate
Do not debate when all materially relevant positions agree and evidence is strong. Trigger debate when:
- conclusions conflict;
- confidence is high but evidence conflicts;
- pivotal unknowns exist;
- Red Team finds a plausible failure mode;
- Customer Skeptic or Moat Auditor issues a commercial blocker.

### P5 — Consensus-Free Decision
The Chair must not use consensus as the objective. A minority position may win if it has stronger evidence/domain authority.

### P6 — Decision Expiry
Material decisions get a review trigger:
- date;
- metric threshold;
- new evidence class;
- failed assumption;
- market/technology change.

Council Pulse uses these triggers.

### P7 — Premortem Gate
Before SELECTED or irreversible execution, assume the project failed and list the most plausible causes. Convert the top causes into tests or safeguards.

### P8 — Commitment Evidence Ladder
Commercial validation evidence, weakest to strongest:
1. praise / stated interest;
2. contact details / waitlist;
3. meeting / workflow disclosure;
4. internal time or data access;
5. LOI with concrete obligations;
6. deposit / paid pilot / purchase.

Do not label levels 1–2 as willingness-to-pay validation.

### P9 — Foundation Model Uplift Test
For AI-enabled ventures, explicitly test whether better/cheaper general AI strengthens or destroys the venture.

### P10 — Anti-Bureaucracy Budget
Each governance mechanism must justify its cost. LIGHT stays lightweight. COUNCIL uses only decision-changing voices. ARENA gets the full protocol. Remove any field/process that repeatedly fails to change decisions.

P7 Premortem Gate
P8 Commitment Evidence Ladder
P9 Foundation Model Uplift Test
P10 Anti-Bureaucracy Budget

### Support with conditions
P2 Confidence Field
Condition: confidence must never substitute for evidence or become a voting weight.

## Dissent preserved
- The Execution Reviewer warns against turning every small decision into a matrix.
- The Human Advocate warns that paid commitment can underrepresent users with low purchasing authority even when pain is real.
- The Evidence Lead warns that community anecdotes are useful for failure modes and lived experience but should not overrule authoritative evidence.
- The Chair warns against adding more permanent roles when protocol improvements can solve the problem.

## Council recommendation
ADOPT the protocol changes as Universal Council v0.3, with scope-sensitive activation and no additional permanent core roles.

Do not add more members yet. Improve independence, evidence structure, calibration, and selective escalation first.
