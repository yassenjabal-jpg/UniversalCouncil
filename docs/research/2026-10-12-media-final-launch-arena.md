## 3. Fresh policy evidence — 2026-10-12

### E-POL-01 — Standard YPP availability
Official YouTube Help currently lists Iraq among regions where creators can sign up for the standard YouTube Partner Program.

Source:
https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7101720

### E-POL-02 — Expanded YPP
The current expanded-YPP country list does not include Iraq.

Source:
https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13429240

### E-POL-03 — Current ad/Premium entry thresholds
Current standard entry for ad/Premium revenue sharing remains:
- 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 qualified public watch hours in the prior 12 months; or
- 1,000 subscribers + 10 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days.

Source:
https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851

### E-POL-04 — Announced 2027 threshold change
YouTube has announced that, for new applicants from 2027-02-01, ad/Premium entry thresholds will become:
- 1,000 subscribers + 8,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days; or
- 1,000 subscribers + 20 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days.

Creators already in YPP are not removed solely for being below the new entry thresholds.

Source:
https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009

### E-POL-05 — Inauthentic / mass-produced content
YouTube's channel monetization policy explicitly rejects mass-produced, generic, repetitive or template-like content that lacks meaningful variation or original value. The policy applies regardless of whether AI was used.

Source:
https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392

### E-POL-06 — AI disclosure
Creators must disclose meaningfully altered or generated photorealistic content when it makes a real person appear to do/say something they did not, alters a real event/place, or creates a realistic scene that did not occur.

Source:
https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491

### E-POL-07 — Advertiser-friendly context
YouTube states that educational, documentary, scientific or artistic context can affect advertiser suitability and reviewers consider the video, title, thumbnail, description and tags.

Source:
https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9348366

## 10. Final three-video pilot slate

The Council keeps the recovered pilot concept but changes the order to maximize researchability and capability learning.

### Video 1 — "كيف تربح الشركات من شيء تقدمه مجانًا؟"
Purpose:
- prove end-to-end workflow;
- explain one counterintuitive business model;
- strong motion-graphics potential;
- low dependency on proprietary/local data.

Narrative:
free offer → hidden payer/value exchange → unit economics mechanism → risk → when free stops working.

### Video 2 — "لماذا تبيع بعض الشركات بخسارة… وتربح أكثر؟"
Purpose:
- test a second story structure;
- demonstrate loss-leader/subsidy logic without turning into investment advice;
- force clear distinction between temporary strategic loss and bad business.

Narrative:
loss headline → what is actually being subsidized → customer lifetime / ecosystem logic → examples → failure boundary.

### Video 3 — Local-money-flow episode
Working concept:
"أين يذهب كل 10,000 دينار تدفعها في طلب توصيل؟"

This is **conditional**, not locked.

It proceeds only if strong local/regional evidence is available for:
- platform take rate / fees;
- restaurant economics;
- courier economics;
- taxes/fees where relevant.

If evidence is insufficient, substitute a globally sourceable money-flow case rather than fabricate local percentages.

## 11. Production acceptance contract

Each pilot video must pass:

### Editorial
- one clear question;
- one central money mechanism;
- no generic motivation;
- no unexplained jargon;
- conclusion answers the opening promise.

### Evidence
- every material financial number source-linked;
- primary source preferred when available;
- estimate/inference visibly distinguished;
- no invented internal company data.

### Originality
- materially different substance and story structure from other pilots;
- no template-driven mass-production feel;
- AI is a tool, not the content proposition.

### Visual
- every shot has an explanatory or narrative function;
- no filler AI footage;
- stable typography and money-flow visual grammar;
- Arabic text checked manually.

### Audio / narration
- owner's human voice remains default;
- warm conversational Iraqi + MSA balance;
- no news-anchor delivery;
- final audio intelligibility and loudness check.

### Policy
- copyright-safe source assets;
- synthetic disclosure when required;
- title/thumbnail do not misrepresent claims;
- advertiser-friendly context reviewed.

### Operations
Log:
- total production time;
- owner minutes;
- tool credits/cost;
- revision count;
- tool failures;
- manual recovery;
- reusable assets created.
