# Universal Council — Media Production Cell + Audio-Visual Sync ARENA
Date: 2026-10-12
Status: FINAL STRUCTURE AMENDMENT

## Trigger

Owner challenged the Channel Operating System for under-specifying:
- work structure and role distribution;
- background music ownership;
- audio quality ownership;
- sound-effect design;
- picture/sound synchronization.

The review confirmed a structural defect in Media Venture v1.1:
the single AI Visual & Post-Production Producer owned visuals, editing, sound, subtitles and export simultaneously.

That model was too broad for the quality target.

## Decision

Adopt Media Production Cell v1.1 as venture-scoped functions.

These are functions, not necessarily separate headcount and not permanent Council seats.

Required pilot functions:
1. Editorial Director / Showrunner
2. Script & Voice Director
3. Visual Art Director / Frame Curator
4. Motion & Picture Editor
5. Music Supervisor & Sound Designer
6. Dialogue & Mix Engineer
7. Audience Growth & Packaging Operator

On demand:
8. Localization Producer

Shared dependencies:
- Owner
- Venture Operator
- Evidence & Research Lead
- Assurance Reviewer
- Technical Operator
- Finance Controller
- HR & Capability Director
- Domain Specialists

## Why split audio into two functions

### Music Supervisor & Sound Designer
Creative audio responsibility:
- sonic world;
- spotting;
- music brief/cues;
- rights/provenance;
- sound effects;
- atmosphere;
- sonic motifs;
- silence;
- transition sounds;
- AV sync intent.

### Dialogue & Mix Engineer
Technical audio responsibility:
- narration cleanup/edit;
- noise/EQ/de-ess;
- gain/automation;
- music ducking;
- stems;
- final mix/master;
- loudness/peak control;
- audio QC;
- multilingual mix parity.

The first asks: What should we hear, where, and why?

The second asks: Does it sound clean, balanced, intelligible, consistent and technically deliverable?

Neither self-approves the other's critical output.

## Audio-Visual design rule

Sound is designed with picture, not added after picture.

New sequence:
Story Thesis
→ Script/Voice
→ Visual Treatment
→ Audio Spotting + Sonic Concept
→ Reference Frames
→ Storyboard + Transition + Audio Map
→ Animatic
→ Narration recording/dialogue edit
→ Production
→ Picture Lock
→ Music/Sound Design
→ Mix/Master
→ Audio-Visual Sync QC

## Spotting session

Before Reference Frame Lock and again at Animatic Gate:
Showrunner + Motion Editor + Music/Sound Supervisor + Script/Voice Director decide:
- voice state;
- music state;
- SFX need;
- silence;
- sync anchor;
- transition sound;
- prelap/postlap;
- dynamic intent.

## Picture Lock rule

Final detailed sound design starts after Picture Lock.

Any later picture timing change creates AUDIO_RECONFORM_REQUIRED.

This includes:
- cut changes;
- transition duration;
- inserted/removed frames;
- time remapping affecting cue timing.

## Internal audio targets

These are internal production goals, not YouTube requirements:
- program integrated loudness: approximately -14 LUFS ±1;
- true peak ceiling: -1 dBTP;
- documentary loudness-range guideline: roughly 6–10 LU;
- intentional AV sync hits: ±1 frame;
- ordinary action SFX: ±1–2 frames unless deliberately offset;
- localized mixes: aim for perceived loudness within ±1 LU of Arabic master where practical.

Narration remains the priority.
Music automation/arrangement must create space for Arabic speech.

## Required stems

- DX Dialogue

## Sonic anti-slop rules

Reject:
- wall-to-wall music;
- a riser on every reveal;
- a swoosh on every transition;
- generic trailer booms;
- obvious sidechain pumping;
- identical music beds across unrelated episodes;
- sound that merely describes animation already obvious on screen.

Every episode gets:
- its own sound world;
- at least one unique sonic motif;
- deliberate silence;
- a cue sheet;
- an AV beat map.

## Approval separation

- Motion Editor cannot self-approve Picture Lock.
- Music/Sound Supervisor cannot self-approve final technical mix.
- Mix Engineer cannot approve editorial story.
- Owner approves voice identity/performance, separate from mix QC.
- Same person/tool may execute multiple functions during pilot, but cross-function approvals remain.

## Council verdict

GO — ADOPT THE SPECIALIZED MEDIA PRODUCTION CELL AND AUDIO-VISUAL SOUND SYSTEM.

Reason:
The quality target requires dedicated creative-audio and technical-audio responsibility.
The functions can be collapsed operationally during the pilot, but their responsibilities and reviews must not collapse.

## Pilot review

After three videos HR/Finance review:
- unique contribution of each function;
- audio revision count;
- dialogue cleanup minutes;
- music/sound-design minutes;
- mix/master minutes;
- AV sync defects;
- audio QC defects;
- total production cost/time.

Only then decide which functions can be safely merged.
