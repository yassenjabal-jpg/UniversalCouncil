# The Studio — Benchmark Research Evidence
Date: 2026-10-15
Status: SOURCE-GROUNDED OPERATING-MODEL INPUT

## Purpose

This research supports the active media-production identity **The Studio / الاستوديو** and its five-stage / three-gate operating model. It does not copy any reference organization's private process or headcount. It extracts publicly documented principles that fit an Arabic documentary YouTube studio.

## Kurzgesagt

Source:
https://kurzgesagt.org/what-we-do?visit=videos

Publicly documented sequence:
- research;
- script;
- illustration;
- voiceover + animation;
- music + sound effects.

Relevant principles adopted:
- research and source sheet before final scripting;
- story quality is a separate craft from raw fact gathering;
- visual metaphor/design is a distinct production discipline;
- voice timing informs animation;
- music and SFX are authored parts of the film, not afterthoughts.

Not copied:
- vector-only identity;
- long script-draft count;
- exact staffing or timelines.

## Johnny Harris

Source:
https://bulletpointpodcast.com/howiwrite/johnny-harris-reveals-how-he-writes-you-tube-videos/

Publicly described practices:
- reporting brief;
- multi-week research by a dedicated researcher;
- a two-column script pairing spoken words with visual direction;
- writing and visual direction at the same time;
- progressive post cuts from radio/story cut through rough/fine/audio/picture lock;
- thumbnails and sponsorship work proceed in parallel.

Relevant principles adopted:
- two-column AV script;
- writing for screen rather than prose alone;
- story cut before expensive motion polish;
- packaging begins before final edit.

Not copied:
- four-month production cycle;
- volume of notes;
- fixed per-video staffing.

## Linus Media Group

Source:
https://linustechtips.com/topic/813025-how-our-videos-are-made/

The public behind-the-scenes material demonstrates a multi-role production process rather than one person doing every stage. The public discussion around the process also reflects script review, writing, B-roll, editing and production logistics as separate functions.

Relevant principles adopted:
- explicit handoffs;
- project tracking and naming discipline;
- writer/visual/editor separation where useful;
- production coordination as a real function;
- QC after editorial work.

The Studio does not assume the 2017 workflow is LMG's current exact process; it is used only as public evidence for disciplined handoffs in a high-output YouTube operation.

## Beast Industries / MrBeast

Sources:
https://www.mrbeastjobs.com/
https://www.mrbeastjobs.com/creatives
https://www.mrbeastjobs.com/editors

Current public role structure separates Creative, Production, Post Production, Thumbnail and other functions.

Creative Strategist material explicitly evaluates:
- hook;
- structure;
- pacing;
- characters;
- stakes;
- emotion;
- retention;
- thumbnails;
- titles.

Editor material treats every frame/cut as a decision and asks editors to explain:
- cuts;
- pacing;
- sound;
- structure;
- the problem each decision solves;
- intended viewer feeling.

Relevant principles adopted:
- growth/retention is a real craft function;
- packaging is not decoration added after export;
- editor is a creative storyteller, not a keyboard operator;
- every major edit should solve a story/attention problem.

Not copied:
- spectacle/challenge format;
- scale;
- retention optimization that would override factual integrity.

## Vox Explainer Studio

Source:
https://www.voxmedia.com/2022/10/19/23411609/vox-creatives-explainer-studio-celebrates-fifth-anniversary-expands-to-shorter-social-first-formats

Vox describes the Explainer Studio format as blending:
- research;
- reporting;
- insight;
- personality;
- humanity.

Relevant principle adopted:
- explainer identity should come from the relationship between evidence, insight, voice and design, not from repeating one visual template.

## Corridor Crew

Source:
https://www.actionvfx.com/blog/a-new-vfx-workflow-by-corridor-crew

The documented 2026 Corridor workflow focused automation on repetitive VFX constraints to reduce production time and keep the team lean.

Relevant principle adopted:
- automate mechanical production friction;
- preserve creative judgment for artists/editors;
- build tooling around the actual production bottleneck.

Not copied:
- specific AI keying tools;
- Unreal-based virtual production architecture;
- assumption that their VFX workflow fits documentary editing directly.

## Resulting Studio structure

The benchmark synthesis supports:

1. Studio Management
2. Editorial & Research
3. Visual Design
4. Post Production
5. QC & Delivery

with shared services for:
- evidence/research support;
- risk/rights assurance;
- technical operations;
- finance/cost control;
- Owner authority.

Per episode, these departments form one **Episode Pod**.

## Resulting production flow

1. Development Room
2. Preproduction Blueprint
3. Production Pack
4. Post Production
5. Master QC & Delivery

Existing technical gates remain:
- Preproduction Lock
- Rough Cut Review
- Final QC

No extra committee gates were added.

## Design constraint

The Studio borrows **disciplines and handoff patterns**, not identities, proprietary formats, staffing levels, or exact review counts.
