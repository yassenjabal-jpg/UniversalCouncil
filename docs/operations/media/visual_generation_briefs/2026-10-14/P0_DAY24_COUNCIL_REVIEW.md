## Council synthesis

### What now works as a system
- coherent paper/material vocabulary;
- ivory/charcoal anchors with restrained plum accents;
- strong negative space;
- consistent soft studio shadows;
- multiple composition families rather than one repeated thumbnail template;
- the same episode idea can become hero, macro, diagram, cutout, evidence holder and breathing reset;
- blue is controlled as a material state instead of filling every frame;
- architecture is absent and not needed for recognition;
- the frames imply usable motion: groove build, strip subdivision, cutout crossing, ribbon fold, archive insertion.

### F2 note
The macro frame reads as tactile day/night material and works as a close detail. In production, the Art Director should make the division/groove slightly more explicit when the narrative needs the viewer to understand “one unit separating from another.”

### F3 evidence boundary
F3 is approved precisely because it contains **no invented evidence**. The blank holder demonstrates crop, border, shadow and placement. Real evidence must be inserted later without synthetic alteration of the source content.

### F5 note
The current anonymous paper-cut human proves the layout family, but its mannequin-like visual character is **not** a recurring channel motif. Production preference:
1. real contemporary photography cutout;
2. archival figure when evidentially relevant and rights-cleared;
3. generated anonymous figure only when clearly illustrative and narratively justified.
