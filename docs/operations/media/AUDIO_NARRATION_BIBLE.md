# Audio & Narration Bible v1

## Voice identity

Human owner narration is the default channel identity.

Language:
Iraqi warmth + clear MSA vocabulary.

Character:
warm, curious, reflective, intelligent, lightly humorous.

Avoid:
- TV news voice;
- constant excitement;
- synthetic perfect cadence;
- exaggerated trailer voice;
- whispering every reveal;
- dramatic pause after every sentence.

## Narration dynamics

Opening:
direct curiosity, medium energy.

Context:
calm, clear, slightly slower.

Complex explanation:
slower pace, smaller pitch movement, explicit semantic pauses.

Reveal:
brief energy lift, not shouting.

Reflection:
lower energy, more space.

Ending:
open, confident, unhurried.

## Direction notation

Emotion tags describe intention:
[فضول هادئ]
[سؤال حقيقي]
[سر صغير]
[دهشة مضبوطة]
[ابتسامة خفيفة]
[جدية]
[تأمل]
[تأكيد]
[تحذير هادئ]
[همس خفيف]
[تسارع]
[خاتمة مفتوحة]

Pitch:
↑ rise
↓ fall
→ level

Emphasis:
bold only 1–2 words per sentence by default.

Pauses:
use milliseconds in recording sheet when timing is important, otherwise semantic labels:
short / medium / long.

## Music system

Music has states, not one continuous track:
M0 silence/room
M1 curiosity
M2 motion/build
M3 explanation bed
M4 tension
M5 reveal
M6 reflection
M7 resolution

Music must not explain emotion the story has not earned.

## SFX

Use SFX for:
- physical object movement;
- diagram activation;
- page/line reveal;
- scale shift;
- transition anchor.

Do not add a sound to every text animation.

## Mix principles

- narration always intelligible;
- music ducks naturally under dense explanation;
- no clipping;
- consistent loudness across episodes;
- headphones + speaker check;
- Arabic consonants/sibilance checked;
- multilingual dubs checked independently.

## Recording continuity

Record:
- same mic/room where possible;
- room tone;
- pronunciation list;
- alternate takes for high-emotion lines;
- pickup markers linked to script row IDs.
