## One-sentence treatment

A single **division line / unit boundary** migrates from human interaction to macro material, to Egyptian evidence, to technical drawing, to equalized graphic units, while cobalt and ivory reset the rhythm.

## What makes this different from the rejected lean pilot

The pilot is not built from centered captions over repeated diagrams.

Continuity comes from one motif changing medium:
- human/object action;
- macro material;
- real archaeological evidence;
- line/technical drawing;
- halftone/print field;
- deterministic Arabic typography;
- clean sculptural/graphic closing state.

## Required media families

1. real/illustrative human interaction;
2. macro material/object;
3. authentic evidence;
4. native line/vector diagram;
5. halftone/print treatment;
6. typography-led editorial field.

No more than two consecutive beats may share the same dominant medium.

## Structural color

- cobalt/azure: recurring reset field;
- ivory/off-white: breathing/evidence field;
- charcoal: type/line;
- plum/mineral red: episode-specific marker only.

Cobalt must recur meaningfully; it must not become a full-video default.

## Typography

Arabic title and synthesis text are native editable AE text, not generated pixels.

Required designed type moments:
- opening question;
- “اثنتا عشرة + اثنتا عشرة” concept;
- closing synthesis.

Typography changes scale and hierarchy; it must not behave like subtitles.

## Motion contract

Every beat requires:
- entry state;
- active event;
- exit state;
- next-shot connection.

Approved transition families:
- hand/object pull;
- macro takeover;
- cobalt seam reveal;
- evidence-to-trace extraction;
- physical object → technical drawing;
- unequal → equal redistribution;
- color-field reset.

Generic dissolve may support a transition but may not be the primary causal language.
