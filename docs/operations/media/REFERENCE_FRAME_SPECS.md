# Canonical Reference Frame Specifications v1

These 12 frame families are the channel's reusable composition grammar.
They are not finished screenshots to copy unchanged.

## Global rules

Canvas: 16:9.
Safe margins: 6% horizontal, 7% vertical.
Primary negative space: 35–55% for editorial frames.
Language: all text editable.
Hero objects: one by default.
Primary headline: one.
Micro-caption: zero or one.
Texture: subtle paper/print/engraving.
Every frame must declare EVIDENCE / EXPLANATION / ATMOSPHERE / TRANSITION.

---

## F01 — Hero Question

Purpose:
Open a question or major section with one unforgettable object.

Composition:
- 45–60% hero object;
- object may break frame edge;
- headline occupies opposing negative space;
- cobalt or ivory dominant field.

Text:
2–7 Arabic words preferred.

Motion in:
slow reveal, object rise, masked uncover, or 8–14 frame pre-roll before narration.

Motion out:
object becomes wipe/portal into next scene.

Do not:
place three unrelated objects.

---

## F02 — Evidence Document

Purpose:
Prove a factual claim with a traceable source.

Composition:
- source/document occupies 55–75%;
- annotation uses brass/gold or deep forest;
- one source label and date.

Motion:
document slides or unfolds;
specific evidence is highlighted after viewer sees full document.

Transition:
zoom into a word/date/diagram that becomes the next frame.

Rule:
AI-generated material cannot masquerade as the evidence document.

---

## F03 — Editorial Portrait

Purpose:
Introduce a person without news-style lower thirds.

Composition:
- portrait/cutout 40–55%;
- name/context in large negative space;
- historical figures may use engraving/painting cutouts.

Motion:
slow parallax, page reveal, foreground plant/shape occlusion.

Text:
name + one contextual phrase only.

---

## F04 — Scientific Engraving

Purpose:
Explain natural/scientific/historical mechanisms using illustrated material.

Composition:
- ivory/parchment field;
- one central engraving;
- thin cobalt/gold annotations;
- optional botanical/astronomical fragments.

Motion:
line-drawing build, label sequencing, gentle camera drift.

Transition:
illustrated form match-cuts into real photograph/video.

---

## F05 — Mechanism Diagram

Purpose:
Show how something works.

Composition:
- 3–7 nodes maximum;
- one direction of flow;
- consistent arrows/lines;
- no dashboard UI aesthetic.

Motion:
reveal cause→effect in narration order.

Text:
labels only; avoid explanatory paragraphs.

Transition:
follow one moving element into next frame.

---

## F06 — Number / Scale Reveal

Purpose:
Make one number understandable.

Composition:
- one number dominates;
- one visual comparison;
- source ID internally linked;
- no decorative chart clutter.

Motion:
number appears after context, not before.

Rule:
never show an unsourced financial/scientific number.

---

## F07 — Map / Spatial

Purpose:
Explain where, distance, movement, boundary, route or scale.

Composition:
- simplified map;
- only relevant geography;
- route highlighted in gold/cobalt;
- labels localized/editable.

Motion:
route draw, camera push, region isolation.

Avoid:
generic spinning globe.

---

## F08 — Object Macro

Purpose:
Turn a familiar object into the doorway for the hidden story.

Composition:
- object 50–70%;
- close crop;
- texture/light carefully controlled;
- minimal text.

Examples:
clock, cable, button, passport, phone, ticket.

Motion:
macro push or orbit; object becomes transition anchor.

---

## F09 — Historical Collage

Purpose:
Compress historical context without slideshow feel.

Composition:
- 1 hero historical image;
- 1 supporting document/map;
- 1 contextual texture/object;
- asymmetrical magazine grid.

Motion:
layered reveal in narrative order.

Avoid:
rapid piles of unrelated archival images.

---

## F10 — Modern Reality

Purpose:
Ground the abstract explanation in contemporary life.

Composition:
- clean photo/video;
- minimal graphic overlay;
- color grade harmonized to channel palette.

Motion:
natural footage first; graphics only when explanatory.

Rule:
do not cover evidence-rich real footage with unnecessary effects.

---

## F11 — Breathing Reset

Purpose:
Lower cognitive load after dense information.

Composition:
- 60–80% empty cobalt/ivory field;
- one tiny object/phrase;
- no diagram complexity.

Duration:
1–3 seconds depending on narration.

Audio:
music/sound can breathe; narration may pause.

---

## F12 — Ending Reflection

Purpose:
Return to opening object/question with changed meaning.

Composition:
echo F01 but altered by what was learned.

Motion:
slow, controlled, low-density.

Text:
one final idea or open question, not a summary list.

Transition:
fade/cut to end card only after narrative closure.

---

# Reference-board requirement per episode

Minimum: 8 frames.
Target: 12.
Maximum before storyboard lock: 18.

Required in every episode:
- F01 Hero Question
- F02 Evidence Document when factual material allows
- F05 Mechanism Diagram
- F10 Modern Reality or episode equivalent
- F11 Breathing Reset
- F12 Ending Reflection

Each episode must also declare:
- three unique motifs;
- one hero object;
- one unique diagram language;
- one unique transition motif.

If reference frames look like generic AI art rather than one designed visual system, G4 Visual Treatment Lock fails.
