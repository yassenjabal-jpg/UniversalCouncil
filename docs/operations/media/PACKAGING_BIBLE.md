# Packaging Bible v2

Status: CANONICAL
Date: 2026-10-16

## Principle

Packaging starts during W1, not after the video is finished.

**Title + thumbnail + story premise are one click contract.**

The title should create a precise question, contradiction, mystery or promise.
The thumbnail should supply immediate visual tension through one dominant subject.
They must complement each other, not repeat each other.

A package that needs explanatory copy to become interesting is weak.

## Stage A — W1 Owner Title Gate

Before a story may reach the Owner, there must be exactly:
- 3 story candidates;
- 3 distinct story archetypes;
- 1 thumbnail hypothesis per story;
- 2 title variants per story;
- 1 click promise per title;
- 1 answer promise per title;
- 1 Stranger Click result per title.

A W1 thumbnail hypothesis is not final artwork. It is a one-frame proof that the story can be packaged visually.

Required thumbnail-hypothesis fields:
- hero subject;
- visual tension;
- composition;
- optional 0–4 words;
- works without explanatory text = true.

Required title checks:
- concrete rather than category-first;
- clear curiosity gap;
- accurate promise;
- complements the thumbnail;
- Stranger Click Test = PASS;
- YouTube Title Style = PASS.

### Stranger Click Test

Ask:

**Would a stranger with no prior interest in this subject understand that something unusual is happening and feel a reason to click?**

PASS requires:
- a concrete hook;
- an unresolved question, contradiction or consequence;
- no dependence on specialist prior knowledge;
- no newspaper/report-heading feel.

## Story diversity

Different subjects are not enough.

The three Owner-facing candidates must use three different story archetypes, selected from the governing config, such as:
- mystery/investigation;
- hidden system;
- human odyssey;
- engineering impossibility;
- rise and fall;
- scam/heist;
- survival/disaster;
- transformation;
- historical paradox;
- experiment/challenge.

The Owner should receive three different **viewing experiences**, not three versions of the same macro explainer.

## Thumbnail visual language

Use the same channel world:
- cobalt / sky blue / ivory / brass;
- editorial negative space;
- one dominant hero object or face;
- optional classical/scientific element;
- strong silhouette;
- minimal words.

Preferred text:
0–4 words.

Avoid:
- red arrows/circles by default;
- shocked AI faces;
- five unrelated objects;
- tiny explanatory copy;
- repeated identical template;
- fake numbers;
- visual claims the episode does not prove.

## Thumbnail families

T01 Object + Question
T02 Person + Mechanism
T03 Before/After Contrast
T04 Diagram Mystery
T05 Scale / Impossible Number
T06 Map / Hidden Route

The family may repeat across the channel; the concept and composition cannot be mechanically copied.

## Title rules

Strong YouTube title:
- starts from something concrete;
- contains a mystery, contradiction, consequence or unusual action;
- creates a clean information gap;
- can be understood without reading a description;
- sounds spoken and clickable, not institutional.

Weak:
- generic "Everything you need to know";
- vague "The truth about";
- artificial urgency;
- excessive punctuation;
- abstract category + "analysis";
- newspaper section heading;
- report or white-paper phrasing;
- title that requires thumbnail text to make sense.

## Discovery confidence

Current platform-native evidence is part of packaging quality.

If configured YouTube-native discovery is unavailable:
- use an authorized native fallback only if it still observes real YouTube demand/outliers;
- mark materially degraded evidence as **DEGRADED_DISCOVERY_HOLD**;
- do not treat generic news relevance as equivalent to YouTube click evidence.

## Stage B — after Picture Lock

Final packaging craft remains in Production P06:
- thumbnail composite;
- typography;
- materially different packaging variants;
- mobile legibility;
- final title/picture alignment;
- test plan.

Stage B may refine execution but cannot silently change the Owner-locked title. Material title change requires Owner reapproval.

## A/B learning

When YouTube testing is available:
- test materially different concepts, not font/color variants;
- store results;
- do not overfit one test;
- feed durable packaging lessons into the postmortem.

## Multilingual packaging

Do not assume direct title translation is best.

Each language version gets:
- semantic translation;
- click-intent check;
- cultural clarity;
- line-length check;
- thumbnail text localization when needed.
