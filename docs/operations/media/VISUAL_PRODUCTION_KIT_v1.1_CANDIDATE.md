## Identity implementation change

Previous implementation drift:
> Modern Editorial Surrealism interpreted too heavily as tactile paper/card still-life.

Corrected implementation:
> **Kinetic Editorial Surrealism — mixed-media editorial composition linked by episode-specific motif transformation and object-caused motion.**

## Locked deltas

1. Electric cobalt becomes a recurring structural anchor.
2. Paper/card becomes one medium among many, never a default universal material.
3. Continuity is carried by motif transformation, not material repetition.
4. Every shot requires entry/active/exit states.
5. Typography becomes a primary motion/visual medium.
6. Human/object interaction is explicitly allowed and encouraged where narratively useful.
7. Mixed-media diversity becomes mandatory.
8. Transition design precedes final asset production.
9. Evidence is integrated editorially but never generatively rewritten.
10. Reference fidelity and internal coherence are separate gates.

## Runtime target for 35–45 s retry

- 10–12 narrative beats;
- 18–30 meaningful visual events;
- >=4 media families;
- >=3 cobalt-led beats;
- >=3 typography-led beats;
- >=1 credible human/object interaction;
- >=1 evidence insertion;
- >=1 macro bridge;
- >=1 drawing/diagram build;
- >=1 breathing reset;
- no static unchanged frame >3 s except approved exception.
