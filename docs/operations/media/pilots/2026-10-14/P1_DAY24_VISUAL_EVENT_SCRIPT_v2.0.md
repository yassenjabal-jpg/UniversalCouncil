## 1. Story spine

### Question
Why is a day divided into 24 hours?

### Answer boundary
No single person simply “decided” 24.

The modern convention emerged in stages:
- 12 daytime divisions;
- 12 nighttime divisions;
- seasonal/unequal hours;
- later astronomical preference for equal hours;
- consolidation into the familiar 24-part system.

### Episode continuity motif
**DIVISION / SEGMENT / BOUNDARY**

The motif must migrate through different media.

Do not use “paper” as the continuity device.
