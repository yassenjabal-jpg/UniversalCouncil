# 1. Asset classes

## NATIVE-AE
Built deterministically in After Effects:
- cobalt/ivory fields;
- Arabic/Latin typography;
- vector lines;
- unit divisions;
- trim-path animation;
- masks/mattes;
- simple 2.5D cards/strips;
- halftone/dither treatment where practical;
- registration marks.

## REAL-EVIDENCE
Source-faithful historical material.
Pixels may be:
- cropped;
- scaled;
- masked;
- mounted;
- labeled.

Pixels may NOT be generatively rewritten.

## GENERATED-ILLUSTRATION
Allowed for:
- contemporary hand cutouts;
- non-historical tactile/sculptural objects;
- abstract macro materials.

Must be clearly explanatory/illustrative.

## REAL/EDITORIAL-PHOTO
Optional for credible contemporary human interaction if a suitable licensed source is found.

Generated hand is fallback, not evidence.
