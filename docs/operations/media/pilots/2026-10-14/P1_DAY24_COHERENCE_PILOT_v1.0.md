## Editorial answer boundary

Do **not** claim that a single person simply invented the 24-hour day.

Evidence supports a layered history:
- Ancient Egyptian timekeeping divided the daily cycle into twelve hours of day and twelve hours of night; night hours were associated with decan stars.
- These were seasonal/unequal hours rather than our modern fixed 60-minute hours.
- Equal/constant twenty-four-hour divisions were used later in Greek astronomy; modern scholarship commonly associates early adoption of equinoctial hours with Hipparchus.
- The pilot therefore frames the answer as a system that formed in stages, not one decree.
