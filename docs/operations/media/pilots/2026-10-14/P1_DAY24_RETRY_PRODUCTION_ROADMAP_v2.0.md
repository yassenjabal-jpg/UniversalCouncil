Change identity implementation from:
`Modern Editorial Surrealism / paper-centric`

to:
`Kinetic Editorial Surrealism / mixed-media`

# PHASE 2 — Motion/finishing tool qualification

## Objective
Do not design a film that the available tools cannot execute.

## Candidate classes
At least two viable routes where possible:
- restored/verified After Effects + NLE;
- Higgsfield motion/video editing if connected and capable;
- Runway video/multi-shot routes where deterministic enough;
- another specialized compositor/editor discovered through approved plugin workflow.

Descript is a control/reference route only, not a candidate for final motion finishing.

## Benchmark
Every candidate receives the same 4–5 second micro-sequence:

**TEST-T01**
1. cobalt editorial field;
2. object enters/pulls a material strip;
3. strip fills frame as macro transition;
4. reveal into evidence/diagram scene;
5. deterministic Arabic type stays crisp.

## Score weights
- reference motion fidelity — 25
- transition control — 20
- deterministic timing/easing — 15
- mixed-media compositing — 15
- typography quality — 10
- clean 1080p+ export — 5
- iteration speed — 5
- cost — 5

## Hard fails
Reject any route that:
- watermarks final output;
- cannot cleanly export >=1080p;
- cannot time transitions deterministically enough;
- destroys Arabic typography;
- invents/rewrites evidence;
- only applies preset camera motion to still images.

## Deliverables
- primary motion route;
- backup route;
- 4–5s benchmark exports;
- qualification scorecard.

## Exit gate
**P1R-MOTION_TOOL_QUALIFIED**

No shot production starts before this passes.

Estimated effort: 2–4 h.

## Objective
Replace the seven long P1 beats with a visual-event script.

## Target structure
35–45 seconds with:
- 10–12 narrative beats;
- 18–30 meaningful visual events;
- average event interval roughly 1.0–1.5s in active passages;
- 2 intentional breathing moments.

## Suggested DAY24 retry story skeleton

### 00:00–00:03 — Hook
Cobalt field.
Deterministic Arabic headline:
**لماذا 24؟**
One human/object interaction initiates motion.

### 00:03–00:06 — Divide

### 00:06–00:09 — Day / night
Cream and charcoal states become 12 + 12 through animation, not static display.

### 00:09–00:12 — Egyptian night
Shift into real/source-led evidence context or diagram using a shape/macro match.

### 00:12–00:16 — Decans
Typography + real diagram/evidence + controlled animated markers outside evidence pixels.

### 00:16–00:20 — Real evidence

Source label integrated cleanly.

### 00:20–00:24 — Unequal hours

### 00:24–00:28 — Time evolves

### 00:36–00:41 — Answer

**لم يختره شخص واحد.**

# PHASE 4 — Shot Bible + Asset Graph

## Objective
Stop producing independent frames.

Each shot becomes a node linked to the previous and next shot.

## Mandatory shot schema

For each shot:
- Shot ID;
- start/end time;
- narrative function;
- source medium;
- dominant palette;
- composition;
- ENTRY STATE;
- ACTIVE EVENT;
- EXIT STATE;
- transition-in mechanism;
- transition-out mechanism;
- required layers;
- deterministic typography;
- evidence source/rights;
- generation brief if synthetic;
- audio cue;
- fallback method.

## Asset families required
At least four:
1. real evidence/archive;
2. generated conceptual object/image;
3. line/diagram;
4. photography/human interaction.

Recommended fifth/sixth:
5. halftone/print treatment;
6. sculptural/3D-like object.

## Diversity constraints
- max 2 consecutive shots with same dominant medium;
- max 2 consecutive ivory-dominant shots;
- at least 3 cobalt-led beats;
- at least 3 typography-led moments;
- at least 3 extreme scale changes;
- paper texture cannot dominate >40% of runtime.

## Deliverables
- shot bible;
- asset dependency graph;
- rights/evidence ledger;
- generation briefs only after graph lock.

## Exit gate
**P1R-SHOT_BIBLE_LOCK**

Estimated effort: 2–3 h.

# PHASE 6 — Asset production

## Objective
Produce only assets already required by the locked Shot Bible.

## Order
1. real evidence/archive;
2. real/stock photography or cutouts;
3. diagrams/vector layers;
4. typography assets;
5. generated conceptual assets;
6. textures;
7. fallback variants only where a defect exists.

## Generation policy
One shot = one isolated approved generation brief.
Generation must state:
- purpose;
- entry/exit needs;
- subject position;
- negative space;
- compositing needs;
- transparent/mask needs where relevant;
- prohibited artifacts.

Do not ask the image model to render final Arabic text.

## QC
Every asset receives:
- source/fidelity check;
- edge/mask check;
- lighting direction check;
- scale/resolution check;
- AI artifact check.

## Exit gate
**P1R-ASSET_QC_PASS**

Estimated effort: 3–6 h.

# PHASE 7 — Audio spine and spotting

## Objective
Make sound part of the edit design, not a late addition.

## 7A Narration
Use:
- Owner voice if available;
- otherwise approved temporary voice.

Lock narration timing before fine animation.

## 7B Music
Choose/generate one restrained bed with:
- curiosity;
- editorial modernity;
- space for speech;
- no generic trailer swell.

## 7C Spotting
Mark:
- every major transition;
- object contact;
- paper/fabric movement;
- diagram snap;
- archive placement;
- color reset;
- closing resolve.

Not every event requires a sound.
Sound must clarify rhythm, not turn into UI spam.

## 7D Mix target
Pre-master target:
- narration stable;
- music 16–24 dB perceptually below narration depending section;
- SFX subordinate;
- no clipping.

## Deliverables
- locked VO;
- music cue;
- SFX map;
- audio spotting sheet.

## Exit gate
**P1R-AUDIO_SPINE_LOCK**

Estimated effort: 1.5–3 h.

# PHASE 8 — Animatic / Low-cost full sequence

## Objective

## Requirements
- all final timings;
- rough motion;
- all typography;
- all evidence;
- narration;
- temp/final music;
- representative SFX.

Resolution may be preview quality, but:
- no missing shots;
- no placeholder that hides a structural problem.

## Review questions
1. Does visual curiosity renew every 1–2 seconds?
2. Are there breathing moments?
3. Is cobalt recurring without becoming monotonous?
4. Do media families change naturally?
5. Is evidence clearly evidence?
6. Does every transition have a reason?
7. Can the viewer understand narration without staring at text?
8. Does the film feel designed rather than assembled?

## Exit gate
**P1R-ANIMATIC_PASS**

If fail:
return to visual script/shot bible, not random asset regeneration.

Estimated effort: 2–4 h.

# PHASE 9 — Full motion assembly v2

## Objective
Build the real 35–45s retry in the qualified motion environment.

## Assembly rules
- no default preset transitions;
- no static shot longer than 3s without internal evolution;
- use easing intentionally;
- type and image motion share one timing system;
- transitions inherit geometry/material from the outgoing shot;
- evidence pixels remain untouched beyond allowed crop/scale/mask.

## Required motion classes in final pilot
At least:
- 2 object-caused reveals;
- 2 match/shape transitions;
- 1 macro bridge;
- 1 diagram build;
- 1 typography-driven transition;
- 1 color-field reset;
- 1 evidence insertion;
- 1 breathing hold.

## Exit gate
**P1R-PICTURE_LOCK_CANDIDATE**

Estimated effort: 4–8 h.
