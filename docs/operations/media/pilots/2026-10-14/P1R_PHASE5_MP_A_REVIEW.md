### What remains visually weak

- The headline layout is usable but not yet final-brand typography.
- The macro section is too geometrically clean; it needs a more tactile/photographic material phase before becoming the line system.
- The transition from hand panel to macro could use stronger occlusion/foreground continuity.
- Halftone/print texture is underused.
- MP-A proves the grammar, but final production needs richer surface treatment and better easing polish.
