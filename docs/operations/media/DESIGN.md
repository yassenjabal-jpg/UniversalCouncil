# Channel art direction — Kinetic Editorial Surrealism / Mixed Media

## Mission and authority

Explain documentary subjects through an authored, kinetic editorial film. The episode motif moves across media, scale and physical states; brand recognition comes from that visual grammar, not from blue backgrounds or repeating templates.

This document clarifies the already documented art direction; it does not invent a replacement identity. The hierarchy is:

1. Owner's supplied reference `1000061003.mp4` is the primary visual authority.
2. `reference/REFERENCE_TEMPORAL_GRAMMAR_1000061003_v1.0.md` documents its transformations and transition logic.
3. `VISUAL_PRODUCTION_KIT_v1.1.md` supplies the governing craft grammar, under the broad Modern Editorial Surrealism channel identity.
4. `LEAN_VIDEO_PRODUCTION_WORKFLOW.md` governs execution and the three review gates; it cannot weaken the identity to suit an editor or a time target.

## Style foundations

**Required character:** Kinetic Editorial Surrealism / Mixed Media. A conceptual subject migrates between authentic photography/archive, human or object action where meaningful, macro material, cutout/editorial treatment, line drawing, diagrams and sculptural/graphic treatment. Diagrams explain selected moments; they must not become the whole film.

For DAY24, the motif is **division / segment / boundary / unit**, including unequal-to-equal spacing. A division should change material, scale, function or physical state as the narrative evolves. Repeating a row of bars in three scenes does not establish motif transformation.

Surrealism is a meaningful relationship between source media, objects and scale. It is not mandatory classical art, decorative ancient scenery, a luxury blue/gold palette, arbitrary dream imagery or generic AI B-roll. Evidence imagery remains authentic and source-faithful.

## Color and composition

- Electric cobalt/azure is a recurring structural reset field; the kit approximates `#0F57AE–#1B79C9`.
- Ivory/off-white approximates `#F2EFE8`; ink charcoal approximates `#1D1B1A`.
- DAY24's restrained plum/mineral-red accent is topic-specific; source photography keeps appropriate natural color.
- Palette agreement alone is not reference fidelity.
- Use an editorial grid, approximately35–60% negative space where appropriate, varied alignment and hierarchy, and wide/medium/extreme-macro scale changes.
- Centered headline above a centered diagram must not become the universal scene template.
- Frame boundaries can participate in cropping, reveals and motion. Rounded masks, borders and corner radius are composition-specific, not a default card UI.
- Light, material texture and shadow support real depth and source treatment; avoid a repeated drop-shadow/product-card look.

## Typography and RTL

The kit's Arabic direction is Noto Kufi Arabic display and Noto Sans Arabic body, with Source Serif4 for Latin/numerals. Final selection requires actual Arabic shaping, bidi/numeral handling and readability checks. A system-font fallback proves text execution only; it does not lock the channel's typography.

Type is a visual medium: a statement can organize a scene, create hierarchy, change scale, reveal the motif or initiate the next image. It must not be reduced to a small explanatory caption over every diagram. Use asymmetric editorial hierarchy, selective emphasis and intentional breathing space; avoid caption walls and repeating the same centered label treatment.

## Motion and scene states

Each sequence specifies its entry state, active event, exit state, next-shot connection and selected audio cue. This is authored scene work, not a new approval stage.

Prefer object/human reveals, macro material bridges, shape matches, object matches across source media, division/split reveals, drawing-to-object transformations and color-field takeovers. The outgoing subject should cause the incoming scene when the narrative supports it.

Opacity fades, generic dissolves, number appearances and bar scaling are supporting techniques. They cannot be the primary channel motion language. The repository reference deconstruction describes rapid meaningful evolution, with longer holds justified by internal action or documentary reading. Do not convert that observation into a new mandatory prototype ladder or a quota that overrides comprehension.

## Sound and writing tone

Narration stays warm, curious and conversational, with Iraqi Arabic/MSA where the episode requires it. Audio punctuation follows meaningful material or transformation events. A fixed music bed plus three unrelated effects does not establish a sonic scene design. Voice identity, intelligibility, rights and measured output remain separate from artistic reference fidelity.

## Do / do not

**Do:** design one motif's transformations; vary meaningful source/treatment families; use object-caused transitions; let typography shape the image; integrate authentic evidence without altering its content; choose a feasible craft tool after defining the art.

**Do not:** substitute a generic infographic film; treat matching palette and readable Arabic as identity compliance; choose only shapes because they are easiest to automate; reuse identical centered layouts; use technical export success to approve art direction; recreate the old P0/P1/P2 queue or per-frame Council votes.

## Existing-gate expectations

- **Preproduction Lock:** Art Director's one consolidated treatment explains how the motif, media and transitions implement this grammar. Technical feasibility cannot silently replace that treatment.
- **Rough Cut Review:** review the continuous sequence against the documented reference language, separately from internal visual coherence. A consistent but generic motion-graphics cut fails reference fidelity.
- **Final QC:** carry evidenced Art Director reference-fidelity disposition into the independent exact-master review alongside factual, rights, voice, audio and export checks. Technical measurements cannot waive an artistic rejection.

There are still exactly three gates and no ordinary per-frame/prototype approval queue. Art Director retains final creative authority within the locked direction; Owner's explicit rejection is binding for the rejected artifact. The review does not authorize another render or asset generation.
