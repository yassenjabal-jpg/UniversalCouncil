# Deliberation Contract

Material work follows six stages: Frame, Independent Positions, Challenge, Counter-Propose, Synthesize, Decide.

## Frame
State the goal, constraints, scope, stakes and material unknowns.

## Independent Positions
In ARENA mode, relevant specialists record their positions before synthesis.

## Challenge
Each serious option receives an independent review of its strongest counterargument, credible failure case, hidden cost and invalidation evidence.

## Counter-Propose
Provide a safer or higher-value alternative when one is credible.

## Synthesize
Keep only materially different options. Hybrid options are allowed when useful.

## Decide
State the recommendation, reasons, conditions, uncertainty and preserved dissent.

## Convergence
Stop when another round is unlikely to produce decision-changing evidence, objections, options or unknowns.
