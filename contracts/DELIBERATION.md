# Deliberation Contract — v0.3

## Sequence
Material work follows:
Frame → Route → Independent Position → Evidence Matrix → Selective Challenge → Counter-Propose → Premortem → Synthesize → Decide → Expiry/Review Trigger.

LIGHT may compress stages.

## Frame
State goal, constraints, scope, stakes, decision owner, driver, reversibility and material unknowns.

## Independent Position
ARENA requires materially relevant voices to record conclusions before seeing peer conclusions. COUNCIL uses this when disagreement, uncertainty or anchoring risk is material.

Each material position records:
- conclusion;
- confidence 0–100;
- pivotal unknowns;
- strongest contradicting evidence;
- evidence that would change the conclusion.

## Evidence Matrix
For contested options, classify evidence per option as SUPPORTS, CONTRADICTS, NON-DISCRIMINATING or UNKNOWN. Seek discriminating and contradictory evidence, not only confirming evidence.

## Selective Challenge
Deep debate occurs only when disagreement or uncertainty can change the decision. The strongest counterargument, credible failure case, hidden cost and invalidation evidence must be represented.

## Counter-Propose
Provide a materially safer or higher-value alternative when credible. Do not create cosmetic alternatives.

## Premortem
Before SELECTED or difficult-to-reverse execution, assume failure and identify plausible causes independently. Convert material causes into tests, safeguards, monitoring or kill criteria.

## Synthesize
Do not optimize for agreement. Preserve a minority view when it has stronger evidence or domain authority.

## Decide
Record recommendation, decision owner, reasons, conditions, confidence, material dissent, acceptance checks and explicit review/expiry triggers.

## Convergence
Stop when another round is unlikely to produce decision-changing evidence, objections, options or unknowns.

## Anti-bureaucracy
Do not apply matrices, premortems or full debate to low-stakes reversible work unless they can plausibly change the outcome.
