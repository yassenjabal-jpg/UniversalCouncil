# Decision Kernel

COUNCIL and ARENA decisions share one compact record.

Use stable IDs for evidence (E), unknowns (U), proposals (P), objections (O), precedents (R), and specialist findings (D).

A decision record includes: question, user goal, scope, stakes, reversibility, uncertainty, mode, participating roles, evidence, unknowns, proposals, objections, specialist findings, trade-offs, recommendation, dissent, acceptance checks, stop reason, and conditions that should reopen the decision.

Unknowns are prioritized as PIVOTAL, MATERIAL, or BACKLOG. Resolve PIVOTAL unknowns first when they are safely answerable and can change the choice.

Keep one to three materially different serious options. Do not invent alternatives merely to increase option count.

Conclude when the preferred option has received independent challenge, important uncertainty is visible, acceptance checks are clear, complexity is justified, and another round is unlikely to change the result.

When new evidence arrives, reopen only the affected parts unless the root question or scope has changed.
