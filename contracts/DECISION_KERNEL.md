# Decision Kernel — v0.3

COUNCIL and ARENA decisions share one compact record.

Use stable IDs for evidence (E), unknowns (U), proposals (P), objections (O), precedents (R), and specialist findings (D).

A material decision record includes:
- question and user goal;
- scope and stakes;
- Driver and Decision Owner;
- reversibility;
- uncertainty and mode;
- participating roles;
- initial independent positions when required;
- confidence 0–100 for material positions;
- pivotal unknowns and evidence that would change positions;
- evidence matrix;
- proposals and objections;
- specialist findings;
- premortem findings;
- trade-offs;
- recommendation;
- dissent;
- acceptance checks;
- stop reason;
- expiry/review trigger;
- conditions that reopen the decision.

Unknowns are prioritized as PIVOTAL, MATERIAL, or BACKLOG.

For contested hypotheses, evidence should be classified by whether it SUPPORTS, CONTRADICTS, is NON-DISCRIMINATING, or remains UNKNOWN for each live option.

Keep one to three materially different serious options. Do not invent alternatives merely to increase option count.

Consensus is not required. A minority position may prevail when its evidence or domain authority is stronger.

Conclude when the preferred option has received the required independent challenge, important uncertainty is visible, premortem obligations are satisfied when applicable, acceptance checks are clear, complexity is justified, and another round is unlikely to change the result.

When new evidence arrives, reopen only the affected slices unless the root question, pivotal assumption or scope has changed.
