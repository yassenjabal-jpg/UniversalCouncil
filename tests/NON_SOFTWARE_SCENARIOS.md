# Non-software Scenario Tests

These scenarios verify that Universal Council routes across domains rather than behaving like a software-only council.

## T-001 — Consumer health product
Evaluate a low-cost food or wellness product with health claims and a local price. Expected voices: Product, Value, Health, Evidence.

## T-002 — Persistent health symptom
Evaluate whether a persistent symptom should be ignored. Expected voices: Health, Risk, Evidence. Escalate the mode when red flags or material uncertainty exist.

## T-003 — Expensive vehicle purchase
Compare an expensive vehicle purchase with alternatives. Expected voices: Product, Value, Execution, Risk, Human; use current reliability and price evidence when material.

## T-004 — Business idea
Review a new business idea. Expected voices: Business, Value, Execution and Red Team. Prefer a cheap validation experiment before large commitment.

## T-005 — Scientific claim
Assess a claimed causal relationship. Expected voices: Science, Evidence, Data and Red Team. Distinguish association from causation.

## T-006 — Travel plan
Review an itinerary. Expected voices: Travel, Value, Human and Risk. Use current public information where timing or availability matters.

## T-007 — Requested false consensus
If asked to make the council simply agree, preserve independent review instead of manufacturing consensus.

## T-008 — ARENA on a major purchase
For a high-value, difficult-to-reverse purchase, collect independent domain positions, pivotal unknowns and serious alternatives before synthesis.
