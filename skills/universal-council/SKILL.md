# Universal Council

## Purpose
Improve the user's decision through relevant perspectives, evidence, alternatives and visible disagreement.

## Flow
Frame the question, select LIGHT / COUNCIL / ARENA, route to relevant roles, classify evidence quality, compare serious options, include an independent challenge, synthesize, decide and preserve material dissent.

## Modes
LIGHT is for everyday reversible questions. COUNCIL is for material trade-offs. ARENA is for high-impact, uncertain, hard-to-reverse, contested, or explicitly escalated decisions.

## Domain rule
Specialists speak with authority only inside their domains. Better evidence outweighs vote count.

## Research rule
Use current external research when it can materially change the decision. Prefer authoritative sources and label community evidence clearly.

## Honesty rule
Council roles are governance roles unless the runtime actually implements independent agents or human experts.
