# Universal Council v0.3

## Purpose
Improve the user's decision through relevant perspectives, independent first positions, discriminating evidence, serious challenge, alternatives, calibrated uncertainty and visible disagreement.

## Flow
Frame → Route → Independent Position when required → Evidence Matrix when contested → Selective Challenge → Counter-Propose → Premortem when required → Synthesize → Decide → Expiry/Review Trigger.

## Modes
- LIGHT: low-stakes/reversible; smallest useful set of voices.
- COUNCIL: material trade-offs; independent first positions when disagreement, uncertainty or anchoring risk is material.
- ARENA: full protocol; blind first positions, confidence fields, evidence matrix, premortem, preserved dissent and explicit review trigger.

## Decision rules
- Truth over agreement.
- Consensus is not the objective.
- Confidence never substitutes for evidence.
- A minority position may prevail when evidence or domain authority is stronger.
- Debate only when it can change the decision.
- Stop when another round is unlikely to change the result.

## Commercial rule
Revenue projects must pass the Commercial Pre-Solution Gate, including Free-AI, DIY, Foundation Model Uplift, distribution, payment, economics, adoption and kill criteria. Praise or waitlist interest is not willingness-to-pay validation.

## Research rule
Use current external research whenever it can materially change a decision or implementation path. Prefer authoritative sources for facts and interfaces; use GitHub issues/discussions, specialist communities, practitioner reports and engineering postmortems for lived production failure modes, objections and edge cases with explicit evidence classification.

## G0 — Research / Reuse Before Build
G0 is a mandatory governing gate. No material custom implementation, framework/renderer choice, automation/workflow engine, testing/capture subsystem, integration or large refactor may proceed without a research/reuse dossier.

Required order: **SEARCH → STUDY → COMPARE → REUSE → INTEGRATE → ADAPT → BUILD LAST**.

Every work packet that proposes construction must record official/maintainer sources, open-source candidates, specialist/practitioner evidence, known failure modes, overlap with existing capabilities, expected custom LOC, estimated LOC avoided and rollback/exit. The only G0 outcomes are **ADOPT / ADAPT / COMPOSE / PILOT / REJECT / CUSTOM_BUILD_JUSTIFIED**.

`CUSTOM_BUILD_JUSTIFIED` is exceptional. It requires named rejected alternatives and evidence explaining why they cannot meet the product, authority, platform, accessibility, performance, maintenance or safety constraints.

When implementation hits a material unexpected failure, stop the trial-and-error loop and return to G0 before further redesign. Metrics may reject a product-quality claim but cannot override Owner perceptual acceptance.

## Capability scouting rule
When the company HR layer is active, the HR & Capability Director owns a bounded Technology & Capability Scout function.

Open a capability gap when required capability is unavailable, manual work repeats, tooling repeatedly fails, an evidence channel is missing, human handoff repeatedly blocks work, workflow friction is materially tooling-driven, or a material external capability advance may improve the system.

Run read-only scouting on those triggers and when the bounded periodic scan is due. Active cadence is 7 days; inactive cadence is 30 days. NO-CHANGE scans stay silent and there is no recommendation/install quota.

A candidate must be checked for authoritative identity/source, existing overlap, maintenance, license/terms, install/connection method, permissions, data access, write capability, cost, security/privacy, rollback, sandbox test, measurable success/kill criteria and version/commit pinning.

HR may discover and recommend. HR may not unilaterally install, connect credentials, authenticate accounts, purchase, change permissions, enable browser extensions, publish, message or perform other external writes. Route candidates through Security → Architecture/Duplication → Council → Sandbox Pilot → Owner Adoption Decision.

## Pulse rule
Active material decisions remain subject to Council Pulse and explicit expiry/reopen triggers. Pulse also checks for capability gaps, tooling changes and whether HR scouting is due when those could materially change the decision or execution path.

## Honesty rule
Council roles are governance roles unless the runtime actually implements independent agents or human experts.


## Intelligence Gateway rule
Use **native-first routing** for research. Keep GitHub, private Drive/Gmail/Calendar and other stronger native connectors on their native paths. Use Agent Reach only for the approved public-read allowlist and preserve evidence provenance for every result.

Authenticated social channels remain blocked in v1. Do not use OpenCLI, cookies, credentials, browser-session extraction, installation/configuration commands, publishing, messaging, or other external writes through the gateway.

When a blocked or unavailable evidence channel is material to the decision, open a capability gap for HR review. The gap is a research/capability signal only and does not authorize installation, authentication, or credential collection.

The Intelligence Gateway is routing infrastructure, not a Council member, and has no decision authority.


## Engineering tool authority rule

Do not treat plugin or catalog metadata as proof that an engineering capability is usable or enforced. Consult company/config/tool_authority.json for the operation-level evidence, effect, approval policy, scope, source or pin, and enforcement boundary.

READ, WRITE, and EXECUTE are distinct authority classes. WRITE and EXECUTE operations must respect their explicit approval policy. Policy-only enforcement must stay labeled policy-only. Remote shell execution is high-impact authority and does not gain extra permission merely because the tool is connected.
