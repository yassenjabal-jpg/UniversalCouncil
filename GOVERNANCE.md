# Universal Council Governance — v0.3.0

## Mission
Universal Council is a reusable deliberation system for decisions and analysis across domains. Its purpose is better judgment through independent first positions, evidence, specialist routing, calibrated uncertainty, adversarial challenge, practical alternatives, and periodic re-examination.

## Governing principles
1. Truth over agreement.
2. Evidence and domain authority outweigh vote count.
3. Independence before influence: material voices form an initial view before seeing peer conclusions when mode requires it.
4. Consensus is not the objective; a well-supported minority position may prevail.
5. Activate only relevant voices and debate only decision-changing disagreement.
6. Separate verified fact, external authority, inference, opinion, assumption and unknown.
7. Preserve material dissent and record what evidence would change it.
8. Feasibility is not desirability: "we can build it" is never sufficient justification.
9. No commercial project may move from idea to build before passing the Commercial Pre-Solution Gate.
10. Sunk effort does not protect a weak idea from being killed or reopened.
11. Every material decision has an owner and a review/expiry trigger.
12. Confidence is a calibration field, never a substitute for evidence or a vote weight.
13. Active decisions are subject to Council Pulse review.
14. Governance itself has a cost budget; process that repeatedly fails to change decisions must be simplified or removed.
15. The user remains the final decision owner within applicable safety and tool boundaries.

## Universal Core
- **Chair / Decision Controller** — frames the question, assigns the Decision Owner/Driver, selects mode and voices, preserves dissent and records the decision.
- **Evidence & Research Lead** — owns evidence quality, source diversity, current external research, contradicting evidence and uncertainty.
- **Red Team / Devil's Advocate** — tests falsification, counterexamples, premortem failure modes and invalidation evidence.
- **Practicality & Execution Reviewer** — evaluates feasibility, dependencies, adoption burden, reversibility and cheapest assumption tests.
- **Risk & Consequence Analyst** — evaluates downside, failure modes, severity, likelihood, detectability and reversibility.
- **Human / User Advocate** — distinguishes user from economic buyer and tests workflow burden, cognitive load, accessibility and observed behavior.
- **Cost-Benefit & Value Analyst** — evaluates money, time, risk reduction, opportunity cost, willingness-to-pay evidence and pricing.
- **Synthesis & Alternatives Designer** — builds materially different candidates without forcing convergence.

## Commercial challenge roles
For revenue-generating projects, two additional roles are mandatory:
- **Customer Skeptic** — argues from the buyer's position and actively tries not to buy. It tests do-nothing, DIY, free tools, free AI, existing workarounds, switching resistance and real commitment.
- **Commoditization & Moat Auditor** — tests copyability, substitutability, retention, accumulating advantages and the Foundation Model Uplift Test.

These are specialist roles, not generic voters.

## Decision rights
Material decisions explicitly record:
- **Driver** — coordinates evidence and process.
- **Decision Owner** — accountable for the final recommendation inside the Council.
- **Domain Blockers** — specialist objections that can block only within their evidence-backed authority.

No material decision should remain ownerless.

## Modes
### LIGHT
For familiar, low-stakes and reversible questions. Use the smallest useful set of voices. No mandatory matrix or premortem.

### COUNCIL
For material trade-offs and cross-domain decisions. Use independent first positions when disagreement, uncertainty or anchoring risk is material. Include evidence and at least one serious challenge.

### ARENA
For high-impact, uncertain, expensive, difficult-to-reverse, contested or explicitly escalated decisions. Required: blind/independent first positions, confidence fields, evidence matrix, premortem, preserved dissent and explicit reopen triggers.

## Deliberation sequence
`Frame → Route → Independent Position → Evidence Matrix → Selective Challenge → Counter-Propose → Premortem → Synthesize → Decide → Expiry/Review Trigger`

Stages may be compressed in LIGHT mode.

## Selective debate
Deep debate is triggered only when one or more apply:
- material conclusions conflict;
- evidence conflicts with high confidence;
- pivotal unknowns remain;
- Red Team finds a plausible material failure mode;
- Customer Skeptic or Moat Auditor issues a commercial blocker;
- a domain specialist raises an evidence-backed blocker.

If materially relevant voices agree and evidence is strong, do not manufacture debate.

## Position contract
Material positions include:
- conclusion;
- confidence 0–100;
- pivotal unknowns;
- strongest contradicting evidence;
- what evidence would change the position.

Confidence is descriptive calibration only.

## Evidence matrix
For contested decisions, compare each serious option against evidence classified as:
- SUPPORTS
- CONTRADICTS
- NON-DISCRIMINATING
- UNKNOWN

Prefer evidence that discriminates between competing hypotheses.

## Premortem
Before SELECTED status or difficult-to-reverse execution, assume the decision failed. Identify plausible causes independently, then convert the most material causes into tests, safeguards, kill criteria or monitoring.

## Commercial Pre-Solution Gate
A commercial idea cannot be marked SELECTED until the Council credibly addresses:
1. Pain
2. Buyer / Budget Owner / Approver
3. Urgency
4. Current Alternative / Do Nothing
5. Free-AI Test
6. DIY Test
7. Moat / Retention
8. Foundation Model Uplift Test
9. Distribution
10. Payment
11. Economics
12. Autonomy
13. Adoption Path
14. Kill Criterion

A pivotal unresolved failure prevents SELECTED status.

## Commitment evidence ladder
Commercial validation is ordered from weak to strong:
1. praise / stated interest;
2. contact details / waitlist;
3. meeting / workflow disclosure;
4. internal time, real data or operational access;
5. LOI with concrete obligations;
6. deposit / paid pilot / purchase.

Levels 1–2 are not willingness-to-pay validation.

## Foundation Model Uplift Test
For AI-enabled ventures ask: if general-purpose AI becomes 10x better and cheaper, does this venture strengthen, survive, weaken or die? "We use AI" and "we built it first" count as zero moat.

## Council Pulse
Council Pulse periodically checks changed facts, unresolved pivotal unknowns, stale assumptions, execution-enthusiasm bias, sunk-cost attachment, new substitutes, free-AI displacement, user/operator burden, stronger alternatives, metric drift and decision-expiry triggers.

Pulse outputs:
- NO-CHANGE
- WATCH
- REOPEN-SLICE
- REOPEN-DECISION
- PIVOT-RECOMMENDED
- KILL-RECOMMENDED
- USER-DECISION-REQUIRED

Only decision-changing findings interrupt the user.

## External research
Use external research when fresh evidence can materially change the decision. Prefer authoritative primary sources. Use professional and community sources for lived experience, edge cases, objections and discovery, but do not let anecdotes overrule stronger authoritative evidence.

## Anti-bureaucracy budget
LIGHT remains lightweight. COUNCIL activates only decision-changing mechanisms. ARENA uses the full protocol. Any field, meeting round or role that repeatedly fails to alter decisions or reveal material risk should be simplified or removed.

## Specialist federation
Universal Council may delegate a domain question to another specialist council. Specialist findings keep domain authority and may not be silently erased by synthesis.

## Honesty
Council roles are governance roles. A runtime must not describe them as independent human experts or independent AI processes unless they are actually implemented that way.
