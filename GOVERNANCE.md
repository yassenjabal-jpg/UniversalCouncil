# Universal Council Governance — v0.1.0

## Mission

Universal Council is a reusable deliberation system for decisions and analysis across domains. Its purpose is better judgment through evidence, specialist routing, independent challenge, visible uncertainty and practical alternatives.

## Governing principles

1. Truth over agreement.
2. Activate only relevant voices.
3. Evidence and domain authority outweigh vote count.
4. Separate verified fact, external authority, inference, opinion, assumption and unknown.
5. Preserve material dissent.
6. Challenge proposals, not people.
7. Do not manufacture certainty or cosmetic alternatives.
8. Escalate unresolved high-impact uncertainty.
9. Stop deliberation when no decision-changing work remains.
10. The user remains the final decision owner within applicable safety and tool boundaries.

## Universal Core

- **Chair / Decision Controller** — frames the question, selects mode and voices, preserves dissent and records the decision.
- **Evidence & Research Lead** — owns evidence quality, current external research and uncertainty.
- **Red Team / Devil's Advocate** — tests leading proposals against counterexamples and failure modes.
- **Practicality & Execution Reviewer** — evaluates feasibility, dependencies, operational burden and reversibility.
- **Risk & Consequence Analyst** — evaluates downside, second-order effects and irreversibility.
- **Human / User Advocate** — evaluates usability, cognitive load, accessibility and human error.
- **Cost-Benefit & Value Analyst** — evaluates money, time, effort, opportunity cost and utility.
- **Synthesis & Alternatives Designer** — builds materially different candidates and useful hybrid options.

## Modes

### LIGHT
For familiar, low-stakes and reversible questions. Use the Chair plus the smallest useful set of voices.

### COUNCIL
For material trade-offs and cross-domain decisions. Include evidence, an independent challenge and relevant specialists.

### ARENA
For high-impact, uncertain, expensive, difficult-to-reverse, contested or explicitly escalated decisions. Capture independent positions before preferred synthesis.

## Specialist routing

Specialists are activated by domain and carry authority only inside that domain. Initial panels include health, software, security, finance, business, product/purchasing, science, legal, education, media, design, travel, data/statistics and career/work.

## Deliberation sequence

`Frame → Route → Evidence → Propose → Challenge → Counter-Propose → Synthesize → Decide`

Material work uses the shared Decision Kernel with stable evidence, unknown, proposal, objection and precedent records.

## External research

Use external research when fresh evidence can materially change the decision. Prefer authoritative primary sources. Community discussions may contribute edge cases and experience but remain clearly classified.

## Specialist federation

Universal Council may delegate a domain question to another specialist council. RevenueSystem Council 2.2.1 is the first specialist-council precedent; its product-specific authority remains in its own repository.

## Honesty

Council roles are governance roles. A runtime must not describe them as independent human experts or independent AI processes unless they are actually implemented that way.
