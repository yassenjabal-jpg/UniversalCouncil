# Finance

Separate lead, offer, booked sales value, receivable, collected cash, recognized revenue, contribution, and profit.

Primary operational metric: trailing-30-day Net Operating Cash Generated, shown beside unfulfilled obligations, spendable cash, recognized result, customers, and owner effort.

Money uses integer minor units; currencies balance independently. Owner capital is excluded from NOCG. Advance cash remains Deferred Revenue until delivery. Corrections use reversals, not deletion.
