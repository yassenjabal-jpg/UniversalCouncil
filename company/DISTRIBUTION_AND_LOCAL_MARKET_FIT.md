# Distribution & Local Market Fit Gate

A commercial venture cannot enter VALIDATION until it explains how the actual buyer in the actual market is reached.

This gate is downstream of the Market Reality Definition Gate. The company must first know WHERE, WHO, MONEY and BEHAVIOR for the target segment before choosing distribution.

Tool availability never selects the market channel.

## Required evidence
For each buyer segment record:
1. economic buyer and decision maker;
2. where the buyer actually spends attention;
3. how new commercial approaches normally begin;
4. first-touch channel;
5. conversation channel;
6. formal-document channel;
7. normal response window;
8. fallback channel;
9. local-market evidence supporting those choices;
10. whether the company can actually operate each channel.

A missing connector is CAPABILITY_GAP, not evidence that the channel is unimportant.

## No-reply diagnosis
NO_REPLY is not equivalent to NOT_INTERESTED.

Before interpreting silence, diagnose:
DELIVERY_FAILURE / CHANNEL_FAILURE / CONTACT_FAILURE / TIMING_FAILURE / OFFER_FAILURE / INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE / NOT_INTERESTED.

A venture may not count a contact as a market rejection unless delivery, contact, timing and channel are sufficiently supported.

## Local-market rule
Country-, culture- or sector-dependent ventures require local market research before choosing acquisition channels. Formal communication channels and customer-acquisition channels must not be assumed to be the same.
