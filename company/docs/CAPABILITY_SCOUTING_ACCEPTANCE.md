# Capability Scouting Acceptance — HR-001

Status: implementation acceptance criteria

The proactive HR scouting change is accepted only when all of the following are true:

- HR owns proactive capability scouting and a Technology & Capability Scout function without adding a permanent role.
- Event triggers include unavailable capability, repeated manual work, recurring failure, missing evidence channels and material external capability advances.
- Active periodic scouting is bounded to 7 days; inactive periodic scouting is bounded to 30 days.
- Candidate dossiers require provenance, maintenance, license/terms, permissions, data access, overlap, cost, security, rollback, sandbox test, success metric, kill trigger and a version/commit pin.
- HR discovery is read-only by default.
- HR cannot unilaterally install, authenticate, connect credentials, purchase, change permissions, enable browser extensions, write externally, publish or message.
- Authenticated/browser-session candidates require isolation and least privilege.
- Pilot installs may not depend on an unpinned moving branch.
- Security and architecture/duplication review precede Council review and sandbox pilot.
- Owner retains final adoption authority.
- HR is measured on detection latency, coverage, candidate decision quality and realized improvement, not on number of tools installed.
- NO-CHANGE scans do not interrupt the Owner.
