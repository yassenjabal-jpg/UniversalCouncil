- Cost of Delay is mandatory;

- may recommend KEEP / IMPROVEMENT_TRIAL / MERGE / REMOVE_WITHOUT_REPLACEMENT / REPLACE / FIRE_RECOMMENDATION;
- cannot unilaterally fire;
- final workforce authority remains with Owner;
- HR cannot evaluate itself;
- complaints about HR route directly to Owner;
- evidence-backed dissent is protected;
- HR owns work-environment/capability review across skills, plugins/connectors, tools, prompts, context, data sources, testing and automation;
- unresolved capability gaps escalate to Owner.
