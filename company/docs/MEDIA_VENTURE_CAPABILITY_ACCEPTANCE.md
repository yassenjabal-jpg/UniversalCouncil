## Pilot gate
A 3-video production pilot is mandatory before scale.

Minimum pilot metrics:
- production time per video;
- owner/human minutes per video;
- tool cost per video;
- cost per published minute;
- revision count;
- factual/policy defects;
- packaging quality.

If the videos are separately authorized and published, also measure:
- CTR;
- first-30-second retention;
- average percentage viewed;
- watch time;
- subscribers per video;
- returning viewers.

After the 3-video pilot HR performs a workforce/capability review.
After 10 published videos, specialist splits may be proposed only if measured bottlenecks justify them.
