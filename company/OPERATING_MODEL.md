# Operating Model

Venture maturity: IDEA → DISCOVERY → VALIDATION → PILOT → FIRST_PAYMENT → REPEATABILITY → SCALE.

Company operation, health, maturity, and autonomy are separate axes. Initial WIP is one live experiment. New ideas go to backlog until the experiment gate closes or real invalidating evidence appears.

## Governors
- Cost Governor: track direct cash/tool cost plus owner minutes separately.
- Complexity Budget: no new role/process without a measured bottleneck and target metric.
- Meta-work alarm: governance/planning effort above 20% in two weekly windows triggers removal of discretionary ceremony.
- Evidence freshness: time-sensitive evidence must be revalidated before use.
- Payment Readiness Gate: beneficiary, collection route, fees, settlement/withdrawal, refund handling, and reconciliation must be verified before a paid live pilot.


## Zero-Revenue Founder Mode
Until the first externally verified collected payment, Zero-Revenue Founder Mode is mandatory.

Commercial members must take an explicit position: SUPPORT / OPPOSE / KILL / PIVOT / TEST_NOW. Neutral language is analysis, not a recommendation.

Each material position must include confidence, whether the venture deserves the next 10 operating hours, whether the member would fund the cheapest necessary test if no free alternative existed, the single strongest reason, the evidence that would change the view, and Cost of Delay.

Before first revenue, the default burden is:
`KILL_UNLESS_NEXT_EXPERIMENT_JUSTIFIED`.

## Distribution & Local Market Fit Gate
A venture may not enter VALIDATION merely because the company has a usable tool such as email.

Before VALIDATION, record the economic buyer, decision maker, buyer attention channels, first-touch channel, conversation channel, formal-document channel, normal response window, fallback channel, local-market evidence and capability status.

Tool availability never determines channel choice. An unavailable but market-correct channel is recorded as CAPABILITY_GAP.

## Workforce governance
HR & Capability Director continuously reviews whether active roles earn their organizational cost. HR may recommend KEEP / IMPROVEMENT_TRIAL / MERGE / REMOVE_WITHOUT_REPLACEMENT / REPLACE / FIRE_RECOMMENDATION, but final workforce authority remains with the Owner.

Evidence-backed dissent is protected and cannot be penalized.
