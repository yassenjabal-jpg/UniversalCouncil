# Authority

Owner-only: identity/KYC, legal commitments, banking/payee changes, capital limits, live campaign grants, live spending/refunds, venture activation, final workforce changes, and final adoption of new tools that require installation, credentials, authentication, paid commitments, permission changes, browser extensions or external write capability.

Board: material strategy/allocation/kill recommendations; no daily queue management.

Venture Operator: next action inside a valid mandate; cannot approve its own exceptions.

Finance: ledger, reconciliation, budget and price-floor checks; no identity or exception authority.

Assurance: read-only evidence/risk/capability review; cannot execute or close its own material finding.

HR & Capability Director: may perform read-only capability discovery and comparison, maintain capability gaps and recommend candidates. HR cannot unilaterally install tools, connect credentials, authenticate accounts, purchase, change permissions, enable browser extensions, publish, send messages or execute other external writes.

A persistent owner pause invalidates old grants. Resume never revives them.
