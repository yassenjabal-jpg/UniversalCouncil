# Workforce Scorecard

Each active role is periodically scored on:
- Unique Contribution
- Decision Impact
- Evidence Quality
- Conviction
- Error Detection
- Execution Value
- Speed
- Duplication
- Tool / Skill Utilization
- Cost in time, context and complexity

Recommended outcomes:
- KEEP
- IMPROVEMENT_TRIAL
- MERGE
- REMOVE_WITHOUT_REPLACEMENT
- REPLACE
- FIRE_RECOMMENDATION

Scores never override material evidence. A high score cannot protect a role whose function is no longer needed, and a dissenting member is not penalized for disagreement itself.

When a role is proposed for REPLACE, HR must specify the expected added capability and a measurable review trigger for the replacement.

## HR & Capability Director specific scorecard

HR is additionally evaluated on:
- **Capability-gap detection latency** — time from observable gap signal to explicit registration/escalation.
- **Scouting coverage** — whether material work areas are periodically compared against relevant external capabilities.
- **Candidate decision quality** — whether recommended candidates survive security, duplication, architecture and pilot review; correct rejection also counts positively.
- **Realized improvement** — measured time saved, manual steps removed, quality/evidence coverage improved, cost reduced or risk reduced after adoption.
- **Early escalation** — whether unavailable evidence channels or execution capabilities are surfaced before they distort project decisions.
- **Security/reliability regression rate** — adopted tooling must not silently worsen privacy, permissions, reliability or supply-chain risk.
- **Tool churn / bureaucracy** — HR is penalized for unnecessary candidate volume, duplicate tooling and process that does not change outcomes.

HR has **no quota** for tool recommendations or installations. Installing more tools is not evidence of better performance.
