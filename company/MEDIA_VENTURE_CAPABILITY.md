# Media Venture Capability Layer

The company may operate a media/content venture only with a venture-scoped production team and measured creative/output ownership.

## Minimum production functions

### Editorial Director / Showrunner
Owns:
- idea selection inside the approved niche;
- hook and angle;
- story architecture;
- script quality;
- editorial pacing;
- final-cut editorial approval.

This function is not the same as Evidence/Research. Correct information can still be bad content.

### AI Visual & Post-Production Producer
Owns:
- storyboard translation;
- AI image/video generation;
- B-roll and visual continuity;
- edit assembly;
- pacing implementation;
- sound and subtitles;
- final technical export.

The Technical Operator builds or integrates tools. The Producer owns visual execution and taste.

### Audience Growth & Packaging Operator
Default status: ON_DEMAND until measured need justifies a permanent split.

Owns:
- title and thumbnail packaging;
- first-30-second optimization;
- platform-specific cut-downs;
- CTR and retention review;
- Shorts/Reels/TikTok adaptation;
- post-publication performance analysis.

Commercial/Revenue Function may use the findings but does not replace this media-specific function.

## Shared existing functions
- Evidence & Research: research dossier, source quality, contradiction checks, factual verification.
- Technical Operator: automation, tooling, connectors, media pipeline infrastructure.
- Finance Controller: cost per asset, cost per published minute, monetization and unit economics.
- Assurance Reviewer: copyright/platform-policy risk, disclosure, factual-risk escalation.
- HR & Capability Director: staffing ROI, tool gaps, performance reviews and replacement/merge recommendations.
- Venture Operator: priorities, WIP, experiment boundaries and project-level decisions.

## Pilot-before-scale rule
The company must not declare the media venture production-ready at scale before a 3-video production pilot.

The pilot must measure at minimum:
- production time per video;
- owner/human minutes per video;
- tool cost per video;
- cost per published minute;
- revision count;
- factual/copyright/policy defects;
- packaging quality;
- when published data exists: CTR, first-30-second retention, average percentage viewed, watch time, subscribers per video and returning viewers.

Publishing is not automatically authorized by this document. Existing company operation/autonomy/live gates still apply.

## Scale review
After 3 videos HR performs a capability review:
- KEEP;
- IMPROVEMENT_TRIAL;
- MERGE;
- REPLACE;
- REMOVE_WITHOUT_REPLACEMENT.

After 10 published videos, if publishing is separately authorized, the team must re-evaluate whether scriptwriting, editing, packaging or analytics require a dedicated split.

No permanent specialist is added merely because media companies commonly have one.
