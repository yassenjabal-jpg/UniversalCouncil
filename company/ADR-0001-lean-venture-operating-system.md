# ADR-0001 — Lean Venture Operating System

## Decision
Adopt a lean hybrid rather than a centralized AI company, permanent multi-council hierarchy, or agent marketplace.

## Why
The current bottleneck is execution evidence, not lack of titles. Extra permanent agents increase context, coordination, and self-review risk before demand is proven.

## Consequences
Functions split only on measured bottlenecks. Finance and Assurance remain separate control functions. The strongest remaining dissent is that even this kernel can exceed the economics of a first USD 20/month milestone; therefore the vertical slice, Cost Governor, and Complexity Budget constrain further work.
