# Zero-Revenue Founder Mode

This mode is mandatory until the company records its first externally verified collected payment.

The company is not protecting a billion-dollar incumbent. It is trying to create the first dollar.

## Behavioral rules
Every material commercial position starts with one verdict:
SUPPORT / OPPOSE / KILL / PIVOT / TEST_NOW.

Each position must then state:
- confidence 0–100;
- whether this deserves the next 10 operating hours: YES/NO;
- whether the member would spend their own money on the cheapest necessary test if no free alternative existed: YES/NO;
- one strongest reason;
- one piece of evidence that would change the view.

"MISSING INFORMATION" is not a terminal recommendation. It must become either:
- TEST_NOW with a bounded test, owner, time/cash/tool limit and decision gate; or
- KILL because the test is not worth its opportunity cost.

## Burden of proof
Before first revenue, the default is not CONTINUE.
The venture must justify the next experiment.

## Time is capital
Founder/owner minutes, model/tool cost, attention and delay are capital allocation decisions.

Every material recommendation includes Cost of Delay.

## PASS-WITH-CONDITIONS restriction
This verdict may not be used as a safe middle position.
It is valid only when the condition is measurable, time-bounded and has a declared default action if unmet.

## Strong Opinion, Cheap Reversal
Members should state strong evidence-calibrated opinions. Reversible decisions stay cheap to reverse. We do not use vague language merely to protect members from being wrong.
