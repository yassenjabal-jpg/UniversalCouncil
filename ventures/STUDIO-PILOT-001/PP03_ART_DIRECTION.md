**Editorial Renaissance Motion**.

## Dominant visual balance

The channel is not dark-first.

Primary field:
**Paper Ivory / warm white**.

Secondary structural fields:
**Cobalt / Clear Blue / Sky Blue**.

Accent:
**Brass Gold**, with limited Deep Forest.

Charcoal is reserved for typography, edge contrast, and small anchors.

Target visual balance on most editorial frames:
- 50–70% ivory / white-light / paper space;
- 15–30% cobalt/blue;
- 5–15% photography/hero object;
- restrained gold/forest accents;
- black/dark only where the actual object naturally contains it.

## Composition

- 16:9.
- 35–55% negative space.
- one hero object.
- one headline maximum.
- one micro-caption maximum.
- alternate sparse editorial frames with grounded modern-reality frames.
- no news lower-third language.
- no dense corporate dashboards.

## Episode motifs

1. **Laptop as editorial portal**
   The laptop changes meaning across the episode rather than becoming a repeated prop.

2. **Cobalt route + brass annotation**
   Routes, evidence links and cause/effect lines use thin editorial strokes over ivory paper.

3. **Layered identity paper fragments**
   Identity/work/location signals appear as paper fragments and portrait cutouts, not software cards.

Unique transition:
**Laptop-edge page turn**.
