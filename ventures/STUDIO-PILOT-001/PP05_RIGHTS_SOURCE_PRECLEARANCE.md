## Core rights decision

Government facts can be used, but government branding is not the visual language of the episode.

DOJ-generated website information is generally public domain unless otherwise indicated; DOJ seals/logos remain protected and third-party-attributed photos/graphics require their own permission.

FBI-provided information not otherwise attributed is public information, but FBI seal/name/initial use must not imply endorsement.

Therefore the Studio will:
- source facts from official DOJ/FBI/IC3 pages;
- rebuild evidence as original ivory/cobalt/brass Data Cards;
- cite source/date in plain text;
- avoid seals/logos;
- avoid embedded third-party photos unless separately cleared.

## Map source

Its vector/raster data is public domain and can be modified commercially.
