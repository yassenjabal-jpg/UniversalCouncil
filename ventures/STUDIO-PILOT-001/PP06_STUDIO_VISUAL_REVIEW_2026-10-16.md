## Executive verdict

**STUDIO VERDICT: NOT READY FOR PP06 VISUAL LOCK**

The eight frames communicate the intended concepts, but the current execution reads primarily as a polished blockout / schematic design pass rather than a finished Editorial Renaissance Motion reference-frame set.

PP07 remains blocked.

The Studio recommends a same-PSD Photoshop refinement pass before the Owner is asked to lock PP06.

## Cross-frame findings

### Critical

1. **Craft level is below Visual Lock quality.**
   The frames rely heavily on flat rectangles, simple bars, placeholder human shapes and schematic laptop outlines.
   The Visual Bible expects a richer editorial image language using real/cleared modern photography, archival/document material, engravings, object macros, paper texture and designed editorial composition where appropriate.

2. **The set trends toward template/dashboard language.**
   Repeated top cobalt bars, equal cards, panels and boxes create a corporate presentation/dashboard feeling.
   This conflicts with the explicit no-dashboard/news-UI rule and weakens episode uniqueness.

3. **Text encoding artifacts are visible.**
   Examples include mojibake such as `â€¢` and `â†’`.
   No visual lock can pass with corrupted visible copy.

4. **Typography does not yet express the locked identity.**
   The current frames are dominated by neutral/bold sans typography.
   Editorial Renaissance Motion calls for a more editorial display hierarchy, with refined Arabic display treatment for viewer-facing Arabic and high-contrast editorial serif behavior for Latin display when Latin is actually needed.

### Major

5. **Paper Ivory / white-light should dominate more consistently.**
   M02 in particular is approximately split between ivory and a large cobalt field; several other frames use broad cobalt headers/panels as a repeated template device.

6. **Texture and materiality are largely absent.**
   The set is too flat/digital for a system whose allowed language includes paper grain, print imperfections, engraving hatch and subtle film grain.

7. **Hero-object discipline is inconsistent.**
   Several frames present multiple equal cards/nodes without a single unmistakable visual hero.

8. **The keyframes are conceptually clear but visually generic.**
   Under the AI-slop test, too many could be transferred almost unchanged to another episode with only label changes.

9. **Arabic-first viewer experience is not demonstrated.**
   Most visible keyframe copy is English. Internal design labels may remain English, but viewer-facing visual treatment for this Arabic episode should be proven before lock.

## Frame-by-frame review

### KF01 — V01.4 — M01
**Concept:** Device here / human elsewhere.

Strengths:
- Core idea reads quickly.
- Cobalt / brass / ivory relationship is recognizable.
- Laptop is correctly treated as the conceptual anchor.

Problems:
- Human and laptop are placeholder geometry rather than editorial subjects.
- No meaningful map/spatial language is visible despite the shot requirement.
- Large cobalt left panel makes the frame feel like a deck slide.
- The result is closer to schematic explanation than a finished F07/F08 editorial frame.

**Verdict:** REVISE.

### KF02 — V02.5 — M02
**Concept:** Freeze contrast: device here, human elsewhere.

Strengths:
- Strongest instant-read contrast in the set.
- Message hierarchy is clear.

Problems:
- Half-frame cobalt dominance weakens the Paper-Ivory-first lock.
- Human remains a generic block figure.
- Caption contains encoding corruption.
- Split-screen treatment feels presentation-like rather than cinematic/editorial.

**Verdict:** REVISE.

### KF03 — V03.3 — M03
**Concept:** Location belongs to the laptop, not the person.

Strengths:
- The logic is understandable.
- Device-location relationship is foregrounded.

Problems:
- The card stack plus full-width cobalt bar reads as dashboard/UI.
- The map/spatial requirement is not actually expressed as a designed map.
- Human portrait is still placeholder geometry.
- Composition lacks an arresting hero object.

**Verdict:** MAJOR REVISE.

### KF04 — V04.3 — M04
**Concept:** 40+ / ~187 / 70+ are different evidence contexts.

Strengths:
- Separation of the three contexts is explicit.
- The warning against merging figures is useful.

Problems:
- Three equal cards create a dashboard/comparison-grid aesthetic.
- F06 Number/Scale grammar prefers one dominant number and one visual comparison.
- There is no strong source/evidence materiality.
- Frame is informative but not visually memorable.

**Verdict:** MAJOR REVISE.

### KF05 — V05.2 — M05
**Concept:** Visible employee decomposes into identity / interview / device-location / work / payment-account.

Strengths:
- Required components are present.
- The relationship can be understood.

Problems:
- Current mind-map/box layout feels corporate.
- Flow direction is not narratively obvious.
- Person is a placeholder.
- Encoding corruption is visible in the explanatory line.
- Needs layered paper fragments / editorial portrait logic rather than generic boxes.

**Verdict:** MAJOR REVISE.

### KF06 — V06.5 — M06
**Concept:** Financial / consequence scale.

Strengths:
- Large-number treatment is legible.
- Forest and brass accents are restrained.

Critical problems:
- Shot requirement contains **three** separate figures:
  - >$5M illicit proceeds
  - nearly $700K facilitators
  - >=$3M victim costs/damages
- The current frame visibly contains only two of them; the approximately $700K facilitator figure is missing.
- No visible source zone is demonstrated.
- Two equal numbers compete rather than one number dominating at a time.

**Verdict:** FAIL / REQUIRED CORRECTION.

### KF07 — V07.4 — M07
**Concept:** Disguise path reverses into evidence path.

Strengths:
- Left/right concept is understandable.
- Central laptop pivot is correctly placed.
- The cobalt-to-brass relationship supports the reversal idea.

Problems:
- The current single still does not sufficiently prove the required before / pivot / after multistate design.
- Generic block endpoints reduce meaning.
- Encoding corruption appears in the trail caption.
- Needs evidence nodes with editorial specificity, not abstract rectangles.

**Verdict:** MAJOR REVISE.

### KF08 — V08.4 — M08
**Concept:** Valid-looking signals remain while the human changes.

Strengths:
- Main message is strong and easy to understand.
- This is one of the best candidates for a memorable section payoff.

Critical problems:
- Matrix requires **six** trust signals: name / interview / device / location / account / work.
- Current frame shows only five; **account is missing**.
- The green marks read as squares, not clearly as looks-valid checks.
- Human-change mechanic is not sufficiently demonstrated in a single state.
- Encoding corruption appears in the bottom line.

**Verdict:** FAIL / REQUIRED CORRECTION.
