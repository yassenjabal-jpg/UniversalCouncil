## Candidate 1 — AI infrastructure financing

Topic:
Who pays for the enormous AI infrastructure build-out, and can the returns justify the debt?

Why now:
A current repricing of AI-linked equities and bonds is putting leveraged infrastructure operators under pressure while hyperscalers continue investing heavily.

Market signal:
Strong global news velocity, active investor/community discussion, and existing Arabic videos on the AI-bubble framing. Competition is high.

Story angle:
Follow the money from debt and capital markets into GPUs, data centers, power and contracted compute, then test whether demand and cash flow can justify the build-out.

Story angle:
