## Editorial Director / Showrunner

**Verdict: REJECT**

The script has a strong premise but spends too long restating the same mechanism:

> laptop in America != worker in America

That insight is effectively explained in R01, R02, R03, then partially re-explained in R04, R09, R10, R11 and the ending.

The viewer understands the core trick early. Repeating it does not create suspense; it creates narrative drag.

Required change:
- explain the mechanism once;
- pay it off clearly;
- move immediately to a new question with new information.

The script must progress by **new discoveries**, not by rephrasing prior discoveries.

---

## Script & Voice Director

**Verdict: REWRITE, NOT POLISH**

Adding Iraqi dialect improved warmth but did not solve the underlying repetition.

Several transitions are verbal rather than narrative:
- “وهنا…”
- “خلينا…”
- “السؤال يصير…”
- “وهنا نرجع…”
- “المفارقة…”

Used occasionally these are conversational. Repeated frequently they become visible scaffolding.

The voice also over-explains concepts the audience has already understood.

Required change:
- fewer explanation sentences;
- more concrete incidents, characters, evidence and consequences;
- Iraqi flavor should appear naturally in connective phrasing, not in every paragraph;
- each paragraph should either reveal, complicate, or pay off something.

## Audience Growth & Packaging Strategist

**Verdict: TITLE PROMISE IS STRONGER THAN SCRIPT PROGRESSION**

The title promises an answer to a bizarre concrete question:
**why were so many laptops inside American homes?**

The viewer should receive:
1. the surprising answer quickly;
2. then discover why the answer becomes bigger/weirder/more consequential.

Current script delays the answer by repeatedly circling it.

Recommended narrative ladder:

1. **The impossible employee** — 30–45 sec.
2. **One real Nashville laptop case** — prove the trick.
3. **Answer the title early** — laptops act as U.S. physical stand-ins.
4. **Now escalate:** one person can be split into identity/interview/device/work/payroll.
5. **Show scale:** homes, companies, money.
6. **Show the weak point:** physical infrastructure creates evidence.
7. **End on the real twist:** what companies thought was one employee was actually a bundle of trust signals.

This gives the viewer an answer, then a bigger reason to continue.

## Red Team

**Verdict: COLD VIEWER FAIL**

Primary objection:

The script often answers a question and then continues discussing the same answer instead of opening a genuinely new one.

A cold viewer may think:
> “فهمت. اللابتوب بأمريكا والعامل بالخارج. شنو الجديد؟”

That thought is dangerous and can appear before minute 3.

The Red Team rejects “new wording” as a retention event.

A retention event must introduce at least one of:
- a new fact;
- a new character;
- a new consequence;
- a new contradiction;
- a new scale;
- a new piece of evidence;
- a genuine reversal.

Rephrasing the geographic-disguise concept is not a new event.

# Studio Consensus

## Root problem

**The script mistakes repetition for retention.**

S3 correctly demanded a new reason to continue every 30–90 seconds, but S5 treated a rephrased explanation as if it were new story movement.

That interpretation is now rejected.

## New rule

A **reason to continue is invalid if it merely restates or reframes information already understood.**

Each retention beat must add material novelty.

Allowed novelty:
- NEW_FACT
- NEW_CHARACTER
- NEW_EVIDENCE
- NEW_CONSEQUENCE
- NEW_CONTRADICTION
- NEW_SCALE
- REVERSAL
- PAYOFF

At least one novelty type must be present in every beat.

## Structural recommendation

Do **not** preserve 12 beats just because S3 had 12.

Rebuild around approximately **7–8 real story movements**:

1. Hook: the laptop is in America, the worker is not.
2. Nashville case: prove the mechanism.
3. Early title payoff: why the physical laptop matters.
4. Escalation: from one laptop to distributed farms / current scale.
5. Bigger twist: one “employee” can be split across multiple people and tools.
6. Stakes: money + corporate access, carefully attributed.
7. Reversal: physical infrastructure that hides them also exposes them.
8. Final insight: an employee is a bundle of trust signals, not one verified fact.

Target runtime should be earned by evidence and story, not padded to 10:00.

If the strongest version is 8:00–9:00, prefer that over a repetitive 10:00.

## Iraqi voice guidance

Use Iraqi flavor lightly:
- شنو
- خلينا
- أكو
- هسه
- ويا

But only where it improves intimacy.

Do not make every sentence dialectal.
Default target:
**clear Arabic + light Iraqi personality + conversational rhythm.**

## Final decision

**S5 = REJECTED_RETURN_TO_S5**

Do not proceed to S6.

Next action:
Rebuild S5 from the story spine, cutting repeated mechanism explanation and requiring material novelty in every beat.
