# BidFit Iraq — Validation Log

## 2026-10-07 — Pilot wave 1

### Hypothesis
Iraqi service companies will value a personalized tender-fit/readiness layer enough to engage, even though generic tender alerts and free AI exist.

### Live opportunity used
GIZ Iraq tender 10050983 — Event Management Services for Marketplace 2026.
- Event: Erbil, 29 November 2026
- Submission deadline: 7 October 2026, 17:00 Iraq time
- Public source: GIZ Iraq tender portal
- Supporting files advertised publicly include ToR, assessment grid, eligibility grid, bid form, invitation and general terms.

### Qualified prospects contacted
1. Capricorn Iraq — public site lists event management and Erbil office.
2. Glitter Communication and Events — public site lists event management and Erbil/Baghdad offices.
3. Prestige for Exhibitions and Conferences — public site lists events, conferences, exhibitions and logistics.

### Outreach
One personalized message per company from bidfit-iraq@agentmail.to.

Offer:
- no subscription;
- no payment;
- one free fit/readiness summary if interested.

### Success signals
Strong:
- asks for the summary;
- asks about future monitoring;
- asks about pricing/service;
- provides company profile or preferred tender categories.

Weak:
- polite acknowledgement only.

Negative:
- no interest;
- already satisfied with existing tender alert system;
- says generic tender alerts are enough;
- no replies after a reasonable sample.

### Council rule
Do not interpret sending messages as validation. Only recipient behavior counts as evidence.

### Next gate
Continue in small personalized batches. Reassess after 20 qualified prospects or earlier if a decisive pattern appears.
