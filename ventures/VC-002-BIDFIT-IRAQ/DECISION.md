# VC-002 — BidFit Iraq

## Status
SELECTED-WITH-CONDITIONS — Round 2 winner

## Job to be done
Help an Iraqi supplier, contractor or service company answer one recurring question:

> Which current tender opportunities are actually worth my time, and what do I need before I can bid?

## Product
A personalized tender-intelligence subscription.

The customer supplies a company profile once:
- sectors and services;
- governorates/coverage;
- company type;
- capabilities;
- certifications/registrations;
- contract-size preference;
- known exclusions;
- preferred organizations.

The system then monitors public procurement sources and returns only relevant opportunities.

Each matched opportunity should include:
- source and issuer;
- deadline;
- fit score;
- why it matches;
- obvious disqualifiers;
- eligibility requirements;
- required forms/documents;
- missing-readiness items;
- next action;
- reminder schedule.

## Initial niche
Iraqi companies that want UN / NGO / international-development procurement opportunities.

Reason: source quality is higher, public procurement pages and supporting documents are available, and deadlines/eligibility are explicit.

## Why free AI is not enough
A free AI assistant can summarize a tender the user already found. BidFit's paid value is the recurring operating loop:
monitor → match to persistent company profile → inspect requirements → flag gaps → remind before deadline.

## Competition
Existing tender databases validate willingness to pay, but generic alerting is already commoditized.

BidFit must not compete on database size. It competes on **company-specific fit and bid readiness**.

## Zero-cost operating model
- web/public sources for opportunity monitoring;
- ChatGPT for filtering and structured analysis;
- AgentMail for delivery and client communication;
- automations for monitoring/reminders;
- GitHub for versioned client-independent operating rules;
- no paid advertising or paid data feeds before monthly revenue exceeds the challenge threshold.

## Revenue hypothesis
A single monthly client should be sufficient to clear the challenge target. Pricing is not finalized until willingness-to-pay validation.

## Human-only dependencies
- receiving money through the user's chosen legitimate payment rail;
- any identity/KYC required by a payment provider;
- customer-provided confidential company documents, if they choose to use them.

## Kill criteria
Kill or pivot if:
1. 20 qualified prospects produce no meaningful interest in fit/readiness analysis; or
2. interested prospects consistently value only generic alerts; or
3. public-source coverage is too incomplete to create dependable value; or
4. fulfillment requires paid data before revenue exists.
