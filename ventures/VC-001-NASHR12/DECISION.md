# VC-001 — Nashr12

## Status
UNDER CHALLENGE — selection reopened by Council Pulse / Pre-Solution Gate

## Goal
Build a zero-upfront-cost project capable of producing more than USD 20 equivalent in monthly revenue.

## Original model
Nashr12 was selected as a productized monthly social-content service for small Iraqi businesses.

## Original offer
- 12 branded feed posts
- 12 Arabic captions
- monthly content calendar
- one revision round
- delivery as ready-to-post files

## Original launch price
39,000 IQD per month for the first client.

## Why it was initially selected
- One paying client could clear the target.
- No paid infrastructure was required to fulfill the work.
- Content ideation and consistency appeared to be recurring pains.
- Fulfillment could be largely operated through ChatGPT + Canva.
- Scheduling could be handled with Metricool for the first brand.
- Local settlement avoided several global creator-platform payout constraints.

## Finding UC-FINDING-001 — Premature Solution Commitment

The initial Council over-weighted feasibility and execution speed and under-tested willingness to pay, free-AI substitution, DIY substitution and defensibility.

The pivotal challenge is:

> Why should a buyer pay Nashr12 instead of using free or already-available AI tools directly?

The original offer does not yet have a sufficiently strong answer.

## Current decision

Nashr12 is no longer treated as selected for launch. Existing assets remain a low-cost prototype only.

The project must pass the full commercial Pre-Solution Gate before further build-out or outbound sales.

Required unresolved questions:
1. Is the pain strong enough to trigger payment?
2. Who is the actual economic buyer?
3. What is the buyer doing today?
4. Why is free AI insufficient?
5. Why is DIY/internal execution unattractive?
6. What recurring value remains after the buyer learns the workflow?
7. Is there a stronger adjacent restaurant problem that AI alone cannot solve?
8. Can the Council operate the result with low user intervention?

## Pivot / kill rule

If the Council cannot produce a credible answer to the Free-AI Test and Moat / Retention Test, Nashr12 should be killed or rebuilt around a different problem rather than defended because work has already been done.
