# VC-001 — Nashr12

## Status
SELECTED — first Universal Council revenue challenge

## Goal
Build a zero-upfront-cost project capable of producing more than USD 20 equivalent in monthly revenue.

## Selected model
Nashr12 is a productized monthly social-content service for small Iraqi businesses.

## Initial offer
- 12 branded feed posts
- 12 Arabic captions
- monthly content calendar
- one revision round
- delivery as ready-to-post files

## Launch price
39,000 IQD per month for the first client.

## Why this model won
- One paying client can clear the target.
- No paid infrastructure is required to fulfill the work.
- Content ideation and consistency are recurring pains for small businesses.
- Fulfillment can be largely operated through ChatGPT + Canva.
- Scheduling can later be added with Metricool free for the first brand.
- Local settlement avoids current payout limitations affecting several global creator platforms.

## Rejected first-wave models
1. Consumer micro-SaaS — distribution and payout complexity before first revenue.
2. Paid newsletter — requires a larger audience and recurring collection.
3. Generic digital downloads — needs repeated sales and global payout support.
4. CV service — feasible but transactional rather than recurring.
5. Affiliate/content site — slower path to reliable USD 20 monthly revenue.

## Stop / pivot rule
If 30 high-quality targeted prospects produce zero qualified conversations, revisit positioning, niche and offer before adding volume.
