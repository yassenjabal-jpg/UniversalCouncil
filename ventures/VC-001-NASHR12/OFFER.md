# Nashr12 — Launch Offer

## Promise
A month of consistent Arabic social content without the owner having to decide what to post every day.

## Package: Launch 39
Price: 39,000 IQD / month

Includes:
- 12 ready-to-post branded social posts
- 12 Arabic captions with relevant calls to action
- 1 monthly content calendar
- 1 revision round
- delivery in an organized monthly folder

## First niche
Restaurants, cafes and visually driven local businesses that already have products/services but post inconsistently.

## Required client inputs
- business name
- logo if available
- brand colors if available
- social links
- menu / service list / offers
- 5–15 existing product or location photos when available
- preferred contact / booking call to action

## Boundaries
No paid ads, photography, video shooting, fake followers, fake reviews or guaranteed sales claims.

## Upgrade after profitability
No paid upgrade is allowed until recurring monthly revenue exceeds USD 20 equivalent.
