# Nashr12 Operations

## Monthly fulfillment
1. Review client assets and previous month performance.
2. Build 12-topic content matrix:
   - 3 product/service highlights
   - 2 educational/helpful posts
   - 2 proof/trust posts
   - 2 promotional posts
   - 2 brand/personality posts
   - 1 seasonal/local post
3. Draft captions.
4. Create visual posts.
5. Quality review: spelling, offers, dates, price claims, brand consistency.
6. Deliver or schedule.
7. Record what performed and adjust next month.

## Zero-cost stack
- ChatGPT: strategy, copy, QA and project operations
- Canva: designs
- Metricool Free: optional scheduling for first brand, up to 20 posts/month
- WordPress.com Free: landing page
- AgentMail Free: dedicated lead/customer inbox
- GitHub: operations and versioned project records

## Owner intervention policy
The system should request user action only when identity, payment receipt, account connection or another human-controlled credential is required.

## KPI
Primary: monthly collected revenue.
Secondary: qualified replies, calls/messages, conversion rate, client retention.
Vanity metrics such as likes are not success criteria by themselves.
